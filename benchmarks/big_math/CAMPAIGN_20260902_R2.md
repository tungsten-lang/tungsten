## Results

### Item 19: reciprocal-quotient admission `BN_DIV_RECIP_Q_MIN` — sweep

`baselines/r2-item19-recipq{256,16,32,64}-…json` (`--accurate`, same
source, knob via CFLAGS; 256 is the shipped default). C lane W/GMP (ns):

| cell | 256 | 16 | 32 | 64 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| div@8 | 0.87 (55.0) | 0.87 (54.2) | 0.88 (55.2) | 0.87 (53.6) |
| div@16 | 0.91 (132.9) | 0.49 (69.3) | 0.91 (132.4) | 0.91 (132.1) |
| div@32 | 0.92 (365.9) | 0.48 (185.6) | 0.47 (187.1) | 0.92 (356.6) |
| div@64 | 0.95 (1110) | 0.56 (638) | 0.53 (613) | 0.53 (606) |
| div@128 | 0.97 (3740) | 0.52 (1950) | 0.48 (1860) | 0.49 (1850) |

With the divisor admitted, the quotient rides the cached Newton reciprocal
(three sightings of the same divisor) and the cells halve. The benchmark
fixture reuses one divisor per cell, so a varying-divisor control decides
whether 16 becomes the default (the ledger's DEEP-13 row: first-sight
caching loses 92% at one use).

Side finding (pre-existing): `bin/tungsten -e "<< Instant.from_seconds(5).seconds"`
fails with "Unknown trait 'BitOrdered'" — `core/instant.w` and `core/time.w`
declare `is BitOrdered`, `core/traits/bit_ordered.w` exists, but the
manifest `core/tungsten.w` has no `auto :BitOrdered` line (the file carries
another session's uncommitted edits). Item 12's spec therefore exercises
Rational, Char and Date only.

### Correctness finding from item 17's evidence: pre-existing isqrt wrong answers

The knob-off control of the 4-limb square-root test failed on about 3% of
3-limb inputs of the form r^2 - 1 / (r+1)^2 - 1 (e.g. the 3-limb value
0x1_bcd306a624c66e80_391bb6c223abea83 returned 0x1_ffffffffffffffff, the
correct root is 0x1_5174283d661951a1). Root cause in `bn_dc_sqrtrem`'s
quotient-overflow branch: when the halved quotient overflows it sets ALL
root limbs to all-ones as if the candidate were B^n, but the candidate is
(s'+1)·B^l, so only the low l limbs step down and the remainder must add
2(s'+1)B^l - 1. Reproducible on pristine main at every width through the
recursive high-part call (`test_isqrt_recbug`: 6000/6000 failures on main,
0 with the fix); the existing fuzzers only generate even limb counts with
random tops and never hit it. `r2_isqrt_sqrtrem_overflow_fix.patch` is
applied ahead of the kernels; all isqrt suites pass with knob on and off
and under ASAN/UBSAN.

### Items 13-15 (decimal TLS workspace + hot alloc), C lane A/B — NEUTRAL

`r2-item13-15-{on,off}-…json`. tostr/fromstr W/GMP on vs off move within
+-8% (tostr@16 0.66 vs 0.60, fromstr@64 0.50 vs 0.53) — inside the noise
floor. Consistent with the agent's finding that the C harness has no
recycler miss (it recycles every result), so the removed per-call mallocs
(8 arena takes/call in tostr's D&C, now 0) do not show in these cells; the
win is for the native lane and real allocating programs. Kept for the
allocation reduction and the strlen removal, reported neutral on the bench.

### Item 13/14 threshold sweeps — defaults stand

tostr `W_TO_S_DC_LIMBS` 24 (default) vs 12 vs 16, W/GMP: @16 0.66/0.55/0.62,
@24 0.81/0.46/0.51, @32 0.65/0.58/0.64, @64 0.66/0.61/0.65. Lowering the
divide-and-conquer cutoff is a loss at every cell: the quadratic 10^19-chunk
writer is faster than the recursive split through 24 limbs on this
architecture. Keep 24.

fromstr `W_FROM_S_DC_DIGITS` 576 (default) vs 288 vs 384, W/GMP: @16
0.33/0.38/0.33, @24 0.38/0.40/0.39, @32 0.42/0.46/0.42, @64 0.50/0.55/0.52.
Within the noise floor (single accurate runs, ~5-15% spread), no decisive
move. Keep 576.

### Item 18: two-row (addmul_2) multiply/square kernels — KEPT (largest C-lane win)

`r2-item18-{on,off}-…json` (`--accurate`, knob `BN_ADDMUL_2`/`BN_MUL1_CSEL64`
via CFLAGS). C lane ns, on vs off: mul@12 32.5/38.5, @16 56.0/60.2, @24
102.6/128.7, @32 175.4/190.8, @48 317.7/374.7, @64 545.6/589.5; sqr@12
30.5/35.0, @24 90.2/103.7, @32 147.9/165.3, @48 295.7/329.4, @64 479.5/531.3.
Every mul and sqr cell 12..64 limbs improves 7-21%; the addmul_2 pass halves
the accumulator load/store traffic of the one-row rungs. W/GMP mul@64 0.55
vs 0.75 overstates it (GMP moved run-to-run); the C-ns 545.6 vs 589.5 is the
real ~7% at 64. The `BN_MUL1_CSEL64` 2x32 carry-select is NEUTRAL (mul1@64
0.99 vs 0.97), so it is dropped; the addmul_2 family is kept. Kernels
verified: 200k-case standalone vs mpn/mpz, all harness fuzzers clean,
knob-off byte-identical to baseline.

### Items 16, 17, 19 verdicts

Item 16 (hgcd2 simulation) — KEPT (big win). `r2-item16-{on,off}-…json`,
W/GMP on vs off: gcd@4 0.71/0.85, @16 0.61/0.89, @64 0.67/0.89, @256
0.68/0.81; lcm@16 0.64/0.86, @64 0.68/0.86. C ns gcd@16 1.36 vs 1.98 us,
gcd@64 7.56 vs 9.89 us — 24-31% at the mid band. The two-limb Möller
simulation extracts ~58-60 bits per full-width pass with one simulator call
vs the 63-bit double-Lehmer's two rounds. Verified: 20k gcd/lcm pairs +
200k simulator exactness cases vs mpz, ASAN/UBSAN clean.

Item 17 (fixed 3-4-limb isqrt) — kernel NEUTRAL, correctness fix KEPT.
`r2-item17-{on,off}-…json`: isqrt@4 1.04 on vs 1.01 off (C ns 50.4 vs 49.7,
GMP-bounded), isqrt@3 0.82/0.84 — the direct kernel does not beat the
divide-and-conquer path at 3-4 limbs, so `BN_ISQRT4_DIRECT` is defaulted
off. BUT the evidence uncovered a pre-existing wrong-answer bug in
`bn_dc_sqrtrem`'s quotient-overflow branch (r^2-1 / (r+1)^2-1 shapes return
all-ones): `r2_isqrt_sqrtrem_overflow_fix.patch`, reproducible 6000/6000 on
pristine main, is KEPT.

Item 19 (`BN_DIV_RECIP_Q_MIN` 256 -> 16) — KEPT. Repeated-divisor sweep:
div@16 132->69, @32 366->187, @64 1110->606 ns. Varying-divisor safety
control (`BENCH_DIV_RECIP_REUSE=1`, cache reset every op): q16 vs q256
137.5/133.0, 368.6/365.0, 1.10/1.10, 3.71/3.73 us — neutral within 3%,
the 3-sighting warmup gate protects varying divisors. (Fixed a stale
harness reference: `bn_div_recip_cache.state` -> `bn_div_recip_caches[i].state`.)
