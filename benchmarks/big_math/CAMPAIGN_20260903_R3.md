### Item 1 (isqrt direct kernel) — REJECTED

The new `bigint_isqrt_4direct` (BN_ISQRT4V2) measured isqrt@4 1.22 on vs
0.99 off (C ns 114 vs 52 — over 2x slower), isqrt@8 0.82 vs 0.77, isqrt@16

Reverted; the profiled "divide dominates" premise held but the replacement
was heavier. isqrt@4 wins WITHOUT it. item 17 (folded in) rejected with it.

## FINAL STATUS: 100/100 achievable, not yet bulletproof

Four accurate quick runs of the kept stack (items 2,4,5,7,9,11), spanning
load 4-76. Two runs (mid, run2) were 100/100; two (final r1,r2) were
97/100. The three cells that flip:
- pow@64: 0.97 / 0.93 / 1.17 / 0.71 — WILD variance from the parallel
  128-limb squaring in the pow ladder stalling under concurrent GPU/compile
  load (3 peer sessions). This is the dominant robustness gap.
- div1@64: 0.98 / 0.98 / 1.00 / 0.99 — thin; item 4 pulled it off the 1.02
  loss but the margin is ~2%.
- isqrt@4: 0.92 / 0.96 / 0.96 / 1.01 — thin; the existing D&C path is at
  parity, item 1's replacement kernel regressed (2x) and was rejected.

So the geomean improved (0.660 vs 0.651 baseline) and every kept item is a
real per-cell win, but a bulletproof all-100 needs: (a) force SERIAL
squaring in the pow ladder for <256-limb intermediates (stabilise pow@64;
round-1 rejected a blanket serial gate for large cells but pow@64's quick
cell is exactly the parallel-stall case), (b) ~3-5% more margin on div1@64
and isqrt@4.

### Correctness finding (isqrt agent, root >= 9): pre-existing overshoot bug
`bn_dc_sqrtrem`'s n=2 overshoot branch returns an all-ones result for
near-perfect-square inputs (r^2-1, (r+1)^2-1, single-bit-root squares) when
the normalized low limb is 0 and `rem_hi + refine_carry == 2` (the `q<<63`
wraps to 0 and misfires the candidate==B^n correction). Round-2's
sqrtrem-overflow fix and item 1/17 cover root <= 8; ROOT >= 9 REMAINS in the
`bn_dc_sqrt_only`/`bn_dc_sqrtrem` large path. The bench fuzzers miss it
(random top-bit-set even-alen operands only). Follow-up: fix the overshoot
handling in bn_dc_sqrtrem/bn_dc_sqrt_only directly (not knob-gated).
