BIGINT POSITIVE ONE-LIMB ADD — EXACT SOURCE CHECKPOINT
=====================================================

Scope
-----

This checkpoint ports the positive heap-BigInt 1-by-1 magnitude arithmetic
from `bigint_add_one_limb_magnitudes` without changing its algorithm or result
policy. Native Tungsten loads both limbs, performs the unsigned add, and records
the carry. The retained raw finisher preserves C exactly:

  carry == 0  -> bigint_finish_one_limb(sum, positive)
  carry == 1  -> exact hot capacity 2; limbs [sum, 1]; signed size +2

Every other sign and width stays on its prior route. This is the exact-port
checkpoint; no native-only arithmetic redesign is included.
