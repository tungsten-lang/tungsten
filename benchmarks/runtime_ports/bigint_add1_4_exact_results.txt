PORT CONTRACT

The admitted positive 4-limb by positive 1-limb shape is a literal native
Tungsten port of runtime.c's BN_WORD_FIXED_CASE(4):

  ldp x4, x5, [src]
  ldp x6, x7, [src, #16]
  adds x4, x4, word
  adcs x5, x5, xzr
  adcs x6, x6, xzr
  adcs x7, x7, xzr
  stp x4, x5, [dst]
  stp x6, x7, [dst, #16]

sets size four directly.  A full carry retains bigint_add_word_into's exact
policy: if the result lacks a fifth slot, allocate five limbs, copy the
already-published low limb, zero limbs 1..3, release the unfinished result,
then publish limb 4 and size five.  No native-only arithmetic or scheduling
change is included.
