PORT CONTRACT

The admitted positive 5-limb by positive 1-limb shape is a literal native
Tungsten port of runtime.c's BN_WORD_FIXED_CASE(5) schedule:

  ldp x4, x5, [src]
  ldp x6, x7, [src, #16]
  ldr x8, [src, #32]
  adds x4, x4, word
  adcs x5, x5, xzr
  adcs x6, x6, xzr
  adcs x7, x7, xzr
  adcs x8, x8, xzr
  stp x4, x5, [dst]
  stp x6, x7, [dst, #16]
  str x8, [dst, #32]
  cset carry, cs

The source wrapper asks the hot allocator for five limbs.  No carry publishes
size five.  Full carry retains bigint_add_word_into's exact construction: if
the result lacks a sixth slot, allocate six limbs, preserve the published low
limb, zero limbs 1..4, release the unfinished result, then publish limb 5 and
size six.  No carry-death, store scheduling, in-place, or other native-only
change is included.
