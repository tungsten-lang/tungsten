PORT CONTRACT

  ldp x4, x5, [src]
  ldp x6, x7, [src, #16]
  ldp x8, x9, [src, #32]
  adds x4, x4, word
  adcs x5, x5, xzr
  adcs x6, x6, xzr
  adcs x7, x7, xzr
  adcs x8, x8, xzr
  adcs x9, x9, xzr
  stp x4, x5, [dst]
  stp x6, x7, [dst, #16]
  stp x8, x9, [dst, #32]
  cset carry, cs

grow-to-seven construction.  There is deliberately no carry-death branch,
copy shortcut, store reschedule, or in-place specialization yet.
