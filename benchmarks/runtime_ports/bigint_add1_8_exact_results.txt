PORT CONTRACT

The current C n==8 arm is not the older unconditional flag chain.  Its
already-measured carry-death schedule is ported literally:

  load all eight limbs with four ldp instructions
  adds limb0; adcs limb1; branch on surviving carry
  common: four stp stores; return carry zero
  rare: six more adcs; four stp stores; cset carry

All loads precede the first store, matching C's in-place safety.  The wrapper
retains the cap-eight hot allocation, normal size-eight publication, and full
carry grow-to-nine construction.  This checkpoint does not further optimize
the existing carry-death schedule.
