PORT CONTRACT

The positive 9..4096-limb by positive one-limb addition is a literal source
port of runtime.c's bigint_add_word_into wide arm:

- allocate exactly alen limbs from the hot allocator;
- add limb zero, then always perform the limb-one carry step;
- ripple only while carry survives beyond limb one;
- copy the untouched suffix with the retained bn_copy_tail implementation;
- on carry-out, preserve the exact allocate/copy/release/grow sequence;
- otherwise publish the unchanged positive size without normalization.

The raw prefix deliberately remains outlined in this checkpoint.  It is the
same C-shaped schedule rather than a new native algorithm.  The copy tail and
finish paths remain the already-tuned C boundaries.  Native call/layout work
therefore belongs to a subsequent, separately measured change.
