BIGINT POSITIVE FIFTEEN-BY-FIFTEEN-LIMB MULTIPLY — EXACT SOURCE CHECKPOINT
=============================================================================

Scope
-----

This checkpoint ports only the distinct, raw-positive-header, positive
fifteen-by-fifteen-limb arm from `bigint_mul_any_routed` /
`bigint_mul_positive_equal`. Native Tungsten preserves C's exact
`bn_mul_eq15` decomposition:

  - the same capacity-thirty-two hot allocation;
  - row zero calls the unchanged generic `bn_mul_1` primitive with length
    fifteen;
  - rows one through fourteen call the unchanged tuned
    `bn_addmul_1_f15` primitive in the same order and at the same result
    offsets; and
  - the result header publishes positive size twenty-nine when limb
    twenty-nine is zero, otherwise positive size thirty.

This is an exact outer-leaf port. Allocation, fixed-kernel selection, row
sequencing, offsets, and publication move to native Tungsten; the fixed C
kernel and row primitives remain the arithmetic implementation. The arm
requires two distinct heap BigInts with raw positive fifteen-limb headers.
Pointer identity remains the existing square route; signs and neighboring
widths retain their former routes. A genuine open-world BigInt `*` reopen
remains observable.

Focused validation
------------------

- the direct source/C differential passes 100,000 products;
- compiled and interpreted public checks pass every named representation and
  route control plus 1,000 randomized products;
- distinct storage, both operand orders, both result widths, high-bit inputs,
  and unchanged operands are covered;
- identity, signed encodings, and neighboring widths remain outside the arm;
- a real BigInt `*` reopen replaces the ordinary and direct seams;
- production, feature-off, diagnostic, kernel-off, and alternate-dispatch
  native self-tests pass;

- the self-hosted compiler reaches byte-identical stage-two/stage-three IR;
- `BN_FROMSTR_FIXED_TOOM=0` and `BN_MUL_N1_FAST=0` configurations build and
  self-test;
