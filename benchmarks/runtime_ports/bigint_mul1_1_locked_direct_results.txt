BIGINT MUL1@1 — CLOSED-WORLD DIRECT-SHAPE FOLLOW-UP
===================================================

Scope
-----

This is the first native-only improvement after the exact positive mul1@1
leaf was committed in bb17e6ad. It does not change that leaf.

When a program declares both `Tungsten.PROTECT_THE_CORE!` and
`Tungsten.LOCK_THE_DOORS!`, an exact `(BigInt BigInt)` multiplication site may
test the committed leaf's complete shape before entering `w_mul`:

  - both values have the exact heap-BigInt tag;
  - the left header size is one, then the right header size is one;
  - values are distinct and both sign-overlay bits are clear.

The checks short-circuit in that order. Wider left operands therefore pay one
tag check and one header load before the unchanged `w_mul` fallback. Header
loads never execute before their corresponding heap-tag proof. Syntactically
obvious `a * a` skips the guard entirely and retains the square path. Open-
world programs, programs missing either contract, mutate-if-unique sites, and
internal runtime arithmetic are unchanged. A compile-time causal switch,
`TUNGSTEN_BIGINT_MUL1_1_LOCKED_DIRECT=0`, restores the prior lowering.

`w_mul` disappears from the profile. The shape guard, multiply, allocation,
publication, and checksum are inlined into the source loop; remaining sampled
cost is result release and allocator TLS lookup.
