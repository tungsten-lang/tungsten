BIGINT POSITIVE THREE-LIMB BY ONE-LIMB MULTIPLY — EXACT SOURCE CHECKPOINT
============================================================================

Scope
-----

This checkpoint ports only the distinct positive heap-BigInt 3-by-1 arm from
`bigint_mul_n1_small`. It deliberately preserves the current C implementation:
the hot capacity-4 allocation; serial low/high 64-by-64 product order; carry
materialization after limbs one and two; unconditional fourth-limb write; and
exact positive size +3/+4 publication. It does not substitute the separately
tuned parallel-product `bigint_mul_n1_tiny` schedule.

The public seam preserves receiver order so an open-world BigInt `*` reopen
retains normal precedence. Only distinct positive 3-by-1 and 1-by-3 heap
shapes are admitted. Pointer-identical squaring, signs, 1-by-1, 2-by-1, and
every wider scalar-word shape keep their existing routes.

This is the exact-port checkpoint. It contains no native-only dispatch,
carry-chain, allocation, or lifecycle redesign.
