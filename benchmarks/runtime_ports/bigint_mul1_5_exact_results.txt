BIGINT POSITIVE FIVE-LIMB BY ONE-LIMB MULTIPLY — EXACT SOURCE CHECKPOINT
============================================================================

Scope
-----

This checkpoint ports only the distinct positive heap-BigInt 5-by-1 arm from
`bigint_mul_n1_small`. It preserves production C's hot capacity-8 allocation,
literal serial mul/umulh/add/carry/store recurrence, unconditional sixth-limb
write, and exact positive size +5/+6 publication. It deliberately does not
substitute a neighboring parallel-product schedule.

The public seam preserves receiver order and open-world BigInt `*` precedence.
Only distinct positive 5-by-1 and 1-by-5 heap shapes are admitted. Squaring,
signs, widths 1..4, and every wider scalar-word shape retain their old routes.
This is an exact-port checkpoint with no native-only integration redesign.
