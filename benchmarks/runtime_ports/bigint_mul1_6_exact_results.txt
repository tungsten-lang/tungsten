BIGINT POSITIVE SIX-LIMB BY ONE-LIMB MULTIPLY — EXACT SOURCE CHECKPOINT
============================================================================

Scope
-----

This checkpoint ports only the distinct positive heap-BigInt 6-by-1 arm from
`bigint_mul_n1_small`. It preserves production C's hot capacity-8 allocation,
literal serial mul/umulh/add/carry/store recurrence, unconditional seventh-limb
write, and exact positive size +6/+7 publication. It deliberately does not
substitute a neighboring parallel-product schedule.

The public seam preserves receiver order and open-world BigInt `*` precedence.
Only distinct positive 6-by-1 and 1-by-6 heap shapes are admitted. Squaring,
signs, widths 1..5, and every wider scalar-word shape retain their old routes.
This is an exact-port checkpoint with no native-only integration redesign.
