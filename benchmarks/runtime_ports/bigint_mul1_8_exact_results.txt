BIGINT POSITIVE EIGHT-LIMB BY ONE-LIMB MULTIPLY — EXACT SOURCE CHECKPOINT
============================================================================

Scope
-----

This checkpoint ports only the distinct positive heap-BigInt 8-by-1 arm from
`bigint_mul_n1_tiny8`. It preserves production C's hot capacity-16 allocation,
eight independently issued products, single straight add-with-carry chain,
paired stores, unconditional ninth-limb write, and exact positive size +8/+9
publication. It deliberately does not substitute a carry-death schedule.

The public seam preserves receiver order and open-world BigInt `*` precedence.
Only distinct positive 8-by-1 and 1-by-8 heap shapes are admitted. Squaring,
signs, widths 1..7, and every wider scalar-word shape retain their old routes.
This is an exact-port checkpoint with no native-only integration redesign.
