BIGINT POSITIVE TWENTY-FOUR-BY-TWENTY-FOUR-LIMB MULTIPLY — EXACT SOURCE CHECKPOINT
==================================================================================

Scope
-----

This checkpoint ports only the distinct, raw-positive-header, positive
twenty-four-by-twenty-four-limb arm from `bigint_mul_any_routed` /
`bigint_mul_positive_equal`. Native Tungsten preserves C's currently selected
top-level difference-form leaf:

  - the same capacity-sixty-four hot allocation;
  - one call to the unchanged `bn_mul_top_diff_small` boundary with length
    twenty-four;
  - the same `bn_toom2_diff24` decomposition and fixed twelve-limb child
    products behind that boundary; and
  - the result header publishes positive size forty-seven when limb
    forty-seven is zero, otherwise positive size forty-eight.

This is an exact outer-leaf port. Allocation, selected-kernel call, and
publication move to native Tungsten; the tuned C difference-form kernel and
its child primitives remain the arithmetic implementation. The arm requires
two distinct heap BigInts with raw positive twenty-four-limb headers. Pointer
identity remains the existing square route; signs and neighboring widths
retain their former routes. A genuine open-world BigInt `*` reopen remains
observable.
