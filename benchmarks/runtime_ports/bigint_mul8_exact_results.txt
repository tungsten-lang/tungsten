BIGINT POSITIVE EIGHT-BY-EIGHT-LIMB MULTIPLY — EXACT SOURCE CHECKPOINT
=====================================================================

Scope
-----

This checkpoint ports only the distinct, raw-positive-header, positive
eight-by-eight-limb arm from `bigint_mul_any_routed` /
`bigint_mul_positive_equal`. Native Tungsten preserves C's exact
`bn_mul_eq8_inline` decomposition:

  - the same capacity-sixteen hot allocation;
  - row zero calls the unchanged tuned `bn_mul_1(n=8)` C primitive;
  - rows one through seven execute the unchanged fully unrolled
    `bn_addmul_8_inline` schedule in the same order and at the same result
    offsets; and
  - the result header publishes positive size fifteen when limb fifteen is
    zero, otherwise positive size sixteen.

This is an exact outer-leaf port. Allocation, row sequencing, offsets, and
publication move to native Tungsten; C's tuned first-row primitive and exact
unrolled add-multiply arithmetic remain unchanged after release/LTO inlining.
The arm requires two distinct heap BigInts with raw positive eight-limb
headers. Pointer identity remains `sqr@8`; signs and neighboring widths retain
their former routes. A genuine open-world BigInt `*` reopen remains observable.
