BIGINT MULTIPLY — TYPED NATIVE-LANE RESULT RELEASE
==================================================

Scope
-----

This is a native-only benchmark-lifecycle improvement after the exact
mul1@1 port (`bb17e6ad`) and its protected+locked direct-shape optimization
(`908f63e6`). It does not change multiplication arithmetic or dispatch.

The native lane formerly released each prior `mul`, `mul1`, and `sqr` result
through the general `w_value_free` heap-kind classifier. These fixtures prove
a narrower contract: both positive operands have the high bit of their top
limb set, so the smallest 1x1 product is at least 2^126. Every iteration result
is therefore a boxed BigInt, while the loop's initial sentinel is nil. The
timed handoff can use the existing `w_bigint_release_dead_raw` boundary, just
as the already-specialized add/sub loops do. Final result cleanup remains
outside the timed interval and still uses the general release.

Counter follow-up
-----------------

Before this change, `tungsten flame --counters` attributed 72.7% of cycles to
`w_value_free` and 25.7% to allocator TLS lookup. After typed release and LTO,
neither symbol appears in the sampled hot path; the loop and raw handoff are
fully inlined. The post-change counter run was dominated by profiler/system
self-accounting attribution, so it is evidence that the old boundaries are
gone, not a trustworthy residual-function ranking.

- `mul`, `mul1`, and `sqr` cover widths 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, and 64;
- the exact 1x1 target promotion wins all 21 pairs;
