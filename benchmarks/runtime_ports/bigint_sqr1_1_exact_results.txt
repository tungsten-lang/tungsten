BIGINT POSITIVE ONE-LIMB SQUARE — EXACT SOURCE CHECKPOINT
========================================================

Scope
-----

This checkpoint ports only the pointer-identical, raw-positive-header,
one-limb arm from `bigint_mul_any_routed` / `bigint_mul_positive_11`.
It deliberately reuses the already committed native 1-by-1 leaf, whose
operation order and result policy are literal copies of C:

  product = (u128)limb[0] * limb[0]
  low     = (u64)product
  high    = (u64)(product >> 64)
  high==0 -> bigint_finish_one_limb(low, positive)
  high!=0 -> exact hot capacity 2; limbs [low, high]; signed size +2

The runtime identity arm has already loaded the raw header. Only `size == 1`
may enter the source seam, exactly matching C: a tag-overlay negative with a
positive header is admitted and squares positive; a negative header remains
on the generic signed C route. Wider squares retain every existing fixed,
Karatsuba/Toom/NTT, redirect, allocation, and normalization choice.

The existing stable mul1@1 seam is reused. Its strong normal definition is
the raw Core leaf, its open-world definition is the final BigInt#* worker,
and its weak stage0 definition is the exact C leaf. No approximate arithmetic
or native-only dispatch redesign is included.
