BIGINT POSITIVE THREE-LIMB SQUARE — EXACT SOURCE CHECKPOINT
===========================================================

Scope
-----

This checkpoint ports only the pointer-identical, raw-positive-header,
three-limb arm from `bigint_mul_any_routed` / `bigint_mul_positive_equal`.
The native Tungsten worker is a literal copy of the current AArch64 leaf:

  - exact hot capacity-8 allocation;
  - the same three diagonal products and three doubled cross products;
  - the linked C `mul` / `umulh` / `extr` / flag-chain schedule;
  - six unconditional limb stores; and
  - positive size 5 when limb 5 is zero, otherwise positive size 6.

The stable seam is dedicated to `sqr@3`. A tag-overlay negative with raw
header +3 is admitted just as in C, while a real negative header and every
neighboring width retain their prior routes. A plain open-world BigInt `*`
replacement still wins over the narrow seam.
