BIGINT SQR@3 — CLOSED-WORLD NATIVE DISPATCH FOLLOW-UP
======================================================

Scope
-----

This is a native-only optimization after the exact three-limb square checkpoint
76009ecd. It does not change square arithmetic, allocation, representation,
normalization, or any C algorithm boundary.

At a syntactic `a * a` site in a program that declares both
`Tungsten.PROTECT_THE_CORE!` and `Tungsten.LOCK_THE_DOORS!`, receiver identity
and method-table immutability are compile-time facts. The generated caller now:

  - proves the exact BigInt tag once;
  - loads the raw signed-size header once;
  - switches directly to the committed exact native sqr@1, sqr@2, or sqr@3
    leaf;
  - enters the unchanged `w_bigint_mul_builtin_exact` boundary for every other
    BigInt shape; and
  - falls back to polymorphic `w_mul` on a stale/non-BigInt type fact.

The dense 1..3 switch was selected only after measuring branch-chain variants.
The size key is the raw header deliberately: tag-overlay-negative values still
have positive raw headers and square through the positive leaves, while true
negative headers retain the exact C path. Open-world BigInt `*` replacement
semantics are unchanged. `TUNGSTEN_BIGINT_SQR3_LOCKED_DIRECT=0` restores the
exact-checkpoint route and is the causal benchmark control.

The protected+locked native caller is 29.8% faster at sqr@3. The same dense
switch costs 3.34% at sqr@1 and 1.50% at sqr@4 in this promotion. These bounded
costs are recorded rather than hidden; both remain below the project's 10%
migration budget. The candidate changes only selection among already-committed
exact leaves, not their arithmetic.

The initial 11-pair screen measured sqr@2 at 0.9587 and sqr@8 at 1.0026.
An 11-pair wide control sweep measured candidate/baseline 0.9959, 1.0028,
1.0021, 1.0129, 1.0060, and 1.0027 at sqr@16,32,64,128,256,1024. The worst
wide control was +1.29%.

Ordinary multiplication controls
---------------------------------

Ordinary `mul1@1,2,4,8,16,32` controls measured 0.9890--1.0010, with the
only wider spread at mul1@16. Ordinary `mul@2,4,8,16,32` controls measured
0.9954--1.0043. The syntactic identity switch does not materially perturb
distinct-operand multiplication.

Validation gates
----------------

  - candidate and feature-off locked-direct specs pass;
  - the focused spec covers positive and overlay-negative three-limb
    identities plus the existing one- and two-limb leaves, wider/dynamic
    identities, signs, stale type facts, and a non-BigInt receiver;
  - retained LLVM contains a dense 1..3 switch and exactly one sqr@3 seam call
    in the candidate, versus none with the feature disabled; both modules
    assemble and verify under LLVM 22;
  - final linked AArch64 contains the committed exact sqr@3 schedule and its
    allocation/publication contract;
  - candidate and feature-off native-lane self-tests pass;
  - two self-host compiler generations emit byte-identical LLVM; and
  - `git diff --check` passes.

One launch-mode `tungsten flame --counters` attempt sampled Apple's property-
list encoder rather than the short-lived workload. It is intentionally not
used as evidence; the causal result is the matched feature-off/on A/B above.
