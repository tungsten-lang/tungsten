PORT CONTRACT

The positive one-limb-by-one-limb subtract-word arm is an exact port of
runtime.c's `bigint_sub1_positive_one_limb` arithmetic:

  diff = a - word
  negative = a < word (unsigned)
  magnitude = negative ? -diff : diff
  signed_size = negative ? -1 : 1

The raw source leaf performs those operations without boxing the full-width
u64 intermediate. The retained finisher preserves C's exact result policy:
i48 demotion (including zero and negative zero), otherwise the bit-48
exact-cap-one hot-slot take, one magnitude store, and signed-size publication.
Only positive effective 1-by-1 subtraction is admitted. Every other size,
sign-overlay, and negative-header shape remains on C.

The native benchmark's subtraction fixture is provably boxed, so its prior
result uses the same typed alias-aware hot handoff already used by the add
lane. Inline-result correctness is covered separately by the focused spec.
