PORT CONTRACT

The admitted positive two-limb by positive one-limb subtraction is a literal
native Tungsten port of runtime.c's bn_sub_word2_a64_fixed schedule:

  ldp x4, x5, [src]
  subs x4, x4, word
  sbcs x5, x5, xzr
  stp x4, x5, [dst]
  cset borrow, lo

The source wrapper asks bigint_alloc_raw_hot for the same cap-two result.
After both limbs are published, the raw finisher repeats
bigint_sub_word_into's exact epilogue: size two if the top limb survives;
otherwise size one followed by bigint_finish_mag_sub, which may demote the
survivor to inline i48 and recycler-release the unfinished box.  The final
borrow is materialized exactly as in C even though the canonical 2-by-1 shape
cannot underflow past limb one.  No native-only arithmetic, scheduling,
allocation, dispatch, or representation change is included.
