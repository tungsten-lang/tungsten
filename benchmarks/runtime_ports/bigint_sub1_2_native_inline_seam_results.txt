DESIGN

The separately committed exact arithmetic leaf is unchanged.  w_sub's
already-proven positive 2-by-1 shape now selects __w_bigint_sub1_2_src rather
than the complete typed BigInt#-(BigInt) worker.  The strong Core wrapper
targets __bigint_sub1_2_raw; C-only and stage-0 links retain a weak definition
that calls bigint_sub_ui_any with the same word.  A plain open-world BigInt#-
reopen still becomes the target of the general and both narrow subtraction
seams.

The first narrow-seam screen removed the typed worker but measured only a
near-tie target win: 0.98569 (3.562 versus 3.633 ns).  Counter attribution
then isolated two integration calls, not arithmetic:

  w_bigint_alloc_hot              23.8% cycles
  w_bigint_sub1_2_finish_raw      13.2% cycles
  __bigint_sub1_2_exact           13.1% cycles
  __w_bigint_sub1_2_src            6.0% cycles

The accepted follow-up gives this shape its own cap-two allocator boundary,
and marks that allocator, the unchanged finisher, and the no-reopen seam
wrapper always-inline.  Whole-program LTO folds them into w_sub.  The reopen
case intentionally keeps an ordinary wrapper attribute so user method bodies
are not force-inlined.  Allocation class, hot-slot policy, exact leaf,
top-limb shrink, i48 demotion, and recycler ownership are unchanged.
