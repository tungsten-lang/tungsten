PORT CONTRACT

The admitted positive three-limb by positive one-limb subtraction is a
literal native Tungsten port of runtime.c's bn_sub_word_a64_fixed case 3:

  ldp x4, x5, [src]
  ldr x6, [src, #16]
  subs x4, x4, word
  sbcs x5, x5, xzr
  sbcs x6, x6, xzr
  stp x4, x5, [dst]
  str x6, [dst, #16]
  cset borrow, lo

The source wrapper asks bigint_alloc_raw_hot for the same cap-three result.
After all three limbs are published, its retained raw finisher sets size three
or, when the top limb vanished, size two followed by bigint_finish_mag_sub.
The latter cannot demote a canonical two-limb survivor but is retained because
it is C's exact shrink branch.  No native-only borrow-death, store scheduling,
allocation, dispatch, or representation change is included.
