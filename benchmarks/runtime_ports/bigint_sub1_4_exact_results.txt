PORT CONTRACT

The admitted positive four-limb by positive one-limb subtraction is a
literal native Tungsten port of runtime.c's bn_sub_word_a64_fixed case 4:

  ldp x4, x5, [src]
  ldp x6, x7, [src, #16]
  subs x4, x4, word
  sbcs x5, x5, xzr
  sbcs x6, x6, xzr
  sbcs x7, x7, xzr
  stp x4, x5, [dst]
  stp x6, x7, [dst, #16]
  cset borrow, lo

The source wrapper asks bigint_alloc_raw_hot for the same cap-four result.
After all four limbs are published, its raw finisher sets size four or, when
the top limb vanished, size three followed by bigint_finish_mag_sub.  No
native-only borrow-death, store scheduling, allocator specialization,
dispatch seam, or representation change is included.
