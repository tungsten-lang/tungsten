PORT CONTRACT

The positive six-limb by positive one-limb subtraction is a literal port of
runtime.c's bn_sub_word_a64_fixed case 6: three pair loads, `subs` plus five
`sbcs`, three pair stores, and `cset lo`.  The cap-six hot allocation and
shrink-to-five finisher retain bigint_sub_ui_any's order and behavior.  No
borrow-death, store scheduling, allocator specialization, direct seam, or
representation change is included.
