PORT CONTRACT

The positive eight-limb by positive one-limb subtraction is a literal port of
runtime.c's bn_sub_word_a64_fixed case 8: four pair loads, `subs` plus seven
`sbcs`, four pair stores, and `cset lo`.  Retained C has no subtraction
borrow-death branch at this width, so source does not add one here.  The
cap-eight allocation and shrink-to-seven finisher are unchanged.
