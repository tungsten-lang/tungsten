PORT CONTRACT

The positive 9..4096-limb by positive one-limb subtraction is a literal
source port of runtime.c's bigint_sub_word_into wide arm:

- allocate exactly alen limbs from the hot allocator;
- subtract limb zero, then always perform the limb-one borrow step;
- ripple only while borrow survives beyond limb one;
- copy the untouched suffix with the retained bn_copy_tail implementation;
- shrink by at most one top limb and use the retained subtraction finisher.

The linked raw prefix leaf is frameless and call-free.  Whole-program LTO
inlines the retained overlap-qpair tail copier into the source worker.  This
checkpoint adds no new arithmetic, allocation, copy, or normalization policy.
