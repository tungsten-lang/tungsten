# Targets of the suite

`r*` is the least rank of a published scheme that is exact over GF(2) (Brent equations checked mod 2 for the files read; see the columns).
Commutative-only ranks and ranks that need division by 2 are listed apart: they are not targets.

KM22 = Kauers and Moosbauer, arXiv:2212.01175, Table 1; KM22b = arXiv:2210.04045; Arai23 = Arai, Ichikawa and Hukushima, arXiv:2312.16960, Table 1 (and their repository Yamato-Arai/adap);
KMW26 = Kauers, Moosbauer and Wood, arXiv:2602.11041, Table 2; Moosbauer and Poole, arXiv:2502.04514 (5x5x5: 93). Sources were read on 2026-09-29.

Formats 4x4x4, 4x4x5 and 4x5x5 have GF(2)-only targets (47, 60, 73). For 4x5x5 the four public tables list 76; 73 is from Arai et al. and Kauers, Moosbauer and Wood.
Lower ranks that are not GF(2) schemes: 2x4x5 has a rank-32 scheme with half-integer coefficients (AlphaEvolve); commutative ranks (3x3x3: 21, 4x4x4: 46, 5x5x5: 85, ...) do not decompose the tensor.
