# Certified GF(2) block extensions, 2026-09-23

The retained exact rectangular certificates support 14 additional full
GF(2) matrix-multiplication tensors whose ranks are numerically below the
corresponding entries in the [Lille table](https://fmm.univ-lille.fr/) as
checked on 2026-09-23. This is a field-specific upper-bound comparison,
not a claim of optimality, cross-field validity, or worldwide novelty.

Each construction uses one certificate-backed parent. `append` adds one
ordinary matrix row/column at cost equal to the product of the other two
dimensions; `double` places two oriented copies in disjoint blocks. The
screen excludes ranks from unprovenanced numerical catalogs. The complete
MFW1 tensors were generated by
`tools/screen_certified_block_extensions.py` and passed its independent
Python parity check and Ruby full-tensor verifier. Exact shared-factor
cleanup found no further rank drop in this batch.
The small [manifest](certificates/certified-block-extensions-20260923/manifest.json)
pins all 14 source and output hashes; the focused test rematerializes every
one without bundling redundant full tensors.
