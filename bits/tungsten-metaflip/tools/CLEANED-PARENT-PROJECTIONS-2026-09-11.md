# Checked projections of cleanup-aware parents

No new retained rank or world record. This follow-up tests the actual
same-rank representations retained by the
[cleanup-aware portfolio](CLEANUP-AWARE-PORTFOLIO-2026-09-11.md), with matched
original-parent controls. It does not restart the native fleet or change seeds.

## One-coordinate deletions

The 333 distinct assessed endpoints, including 18 baseline representations,
give **11,885** single-coordinate deletions. The entire raw projection census
completes, covering **45** canonical target shapes. Native cleanup is then
applied to a bounded shortlist, not to the entire census:

- Four distinct lowest-raw-rank identities per target.
- One deterministic exploratory identity beyond those four, when available.
- The same deletion of each selected variant's original parent, as a control.

This selects **302** contexts. Every context completes native projection,
exact matrix cleanup and full tensor verification. Raw rank is an ordering
heuristic, not a post-cleanup lower bound or dominance rule.

Among 199 non-original contexts with matched controls, **19 are better,
165 tie and 15 are worse**. The largest matched saving is six terms. None
beats the pinned retained bound for its shape. Thus same-rank parent
representations can have different projection utility, but these particular
differences do not establish a new record.

The screen takes about 33 seconds and materialization about 17 seconds on
one low-priority CPU worker, with no GPU. These are run observations, not
a performance comparison.

## Complete second-deletion continuation of the better pairs

Take every one of those 19 better outputs and its matched control after
cleanup. Complete-identity deduplication leaves **27** parents. Enumerate
and clean **all 989** single-coordinate deletions of those parents; there
is no raw-rank shortlist at this stage. All complete, covering 19 target
shapes, in about 46 seconds on one low-priority CPU worker.

Again, **zero new retained ranks**. This closes the declared second-step
family, not arbitrary coordinate restrictions, unselected first-step
projections, basis changes or tensor rank itself. There is no reason to keep
repeating this exact branch with more wall-clock time.

## Independent replay and accounting

The checker rebuilds the complete first-stage census using a separate
sparse-bit projection cache rather than the producer's row-block deletion.
It verifies the exact shortlist and ancestor selection, derives the 27
second-stage parents from the matched outcomes, and independently enumerates
every one of their 989 continuations.

It reconstructs every materialized raw tensor, independently refactors its
shared-factor matrices, checks the entire tensor identity, and runs the
native full verifier on every distinct endpoint. All **64** deliberately
corrupted target representatives are rejected by both checkers. Limited
cleanup/verification is never treated as admission. The audit checks
**2,883** distinct input/raw/output tensors.

Of the 1,291 assessed endpoints, **1,290** are new to the preceding identity
receipt. The cumulative series is now **15,126 complete representations**;
the **38 source-scoped candidate shapes remain unchanged**. Representation
counts are not counts of rank improvements. No main-square or general-field
claim is added.

## Ready next domain: width-six leaves

A separately verified bank now contains one full GF(2) witness for every
canonical triple with coordinates in `{3,4,5,6}`: **20** leaves. The ten
new width-six entries are existing constructions, not new primitive records:

```
336:42 346:54 356:68 366:82 446:73
456:90 466:105 556:110 566:130 666:153
```

The original source bytes, hashes and orientations are retained. Ruby,
independent Python reconstruction and native full verification all pass.
No general-field catalogue rank is silently used as a GF(2) witness.
In particular, the supplied 3x3x6 witness is rank 42, not an unverified
rank-40 algorithm. The bank permits a genuinely broader mixed-width
composition experiment next; that experiment has not run in this follow-up.
These helper leaves are not added to the projection-endpoint rollup above.
