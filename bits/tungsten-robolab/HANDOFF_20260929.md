## 5. Rules and traps

- The arena rules are in `playground/arena/README.md`. Never change an existing trial's seeds, env or metric: make a new trial.
  Edit `playground/arena/champions.json` and `playground/arena/history.jsonl` only under `arena.locked()` (`playground/arena/.lock`).
- Evaluate a copy of your binary. A promotion freezes whatever the path holds at the end of the run.
- Deterministic trials promote on any strict gain; stochastic ones need p < 0.05 on the paired sign-flip test, whose smallest possible
  p is 2^-k for k differing starts. Give a trial enough starts for the gain you care about (that is why the 48-start run trials exist).
- Failed guards and crashes score plus or minus 1e9. `arena.py board` shows the mean over the passing rollouts beside the pass count;
  never read `summary.mean` of a trial with failures directly.
- Label ceiling-class controllers ORACLE in the note (open-loop programs that bypass the contraction-target plus reflex layer).
- Bodies: v2, v3 (tendons), p50 and p95 profiles, elite (unverified magnitudes, provisional), elite2 (literature), v4 (calibrated: quadriceps series
  stiffness 250 to 1000 N/mm, hip speeds 15 to 18.5 and 29 rad/s; `ROBOLAB_CALIB=v4`). Mark results by body. The old 10.2 m/s ceiling was invalid
  (it used an ideal torso stabiliser).
- The checkout is shared with other sessions that commit to `main`. Never `git add` a directory or use the shared index for robolab files;
  use `tools/snapshot_commit.sh` (plumbing onto a side branch).
- The machine is loaded (load average about 50 on 18 cores at 01:15). Wall times run 2 to 3 times slower than in the logs; simulation results are unaffected.
  Use at most 3 or 4 workers per search.
- A worktree build sees the shared checkout's sources unless `BIT_HOME` is set: Erik's `~/.zshrc` exports `BIT_HOME=$HOME/tungsten/bits`, and `use robolab/...` resolves through it before the worktree's own `bits/`.
