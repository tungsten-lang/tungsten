# Fukuchi et al. 2017 running biomechanics dataset

Fukuchi RK, Fukuchi CA, Duarte M (2017). A public dataset of running biomechanics and the effects of running speed on lower extremity kinematics and kinetics. PeerJ 5:e3298. https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.3298
Data: https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.4543435.v5 (CC BY 4.0). Downloaded 2026-09-27: the RBDS*processed.txt files (time-normalised right-leg gait-cycle joint angles, moments, powers and GRF at 2.5 / 3.5 / 4.5 m/s, treadmill, Visual3D) and RBDSinfo.txt. running_curves.json is derived: mean and SD across participants of the sagittal hip, knee and ankle angles in robolab's convention (ankle + plantarflexion) and the median toe-off from the vertical GRF where present.
