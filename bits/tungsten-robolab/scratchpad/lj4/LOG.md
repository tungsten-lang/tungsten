(1) Failures cost about 0.9 m: fell-in-run 7.3-7.5 % = the runner's own falls (unsteered approach: 3.3 %) + steering stumbles (about 2 %) + take-offs that collapse before the plant (about 2 %); fouls 1-2 %; somersaults 2 % (half of them collapsed take-offs, the rest forward angular momentum at take-off: L > 0 at take-off somersaults in 9-100 % of the jumps, pitch > 0.8 rad in 8-11 %). (2) The legal mean is 7.8 m (median 7.95, p90 8.67) against 10.85 m for the one-start program: one fixed open-loop program cannot follow the chaotic per-state optimum, and the programs are not selectable from what the planner measures. (3) F1 arrives 0.22 m/s slower at the line than chain D (about -0.2 m on every jump; chain D has 4.8 % of fresh starts above 8.95 m, F1-based programs 1.0-1.5 %). (4) The take-off foot lands 0.40 +- 0.16 m behind the line (the foul cliff against a sd of 0.16 m; moving it by 0.1 m trades about equal gain and fouls). (5) The landing loses about 0.17 m against the ballistic mark and 0.39 m against the effective mark (95 % of the legal jumps fall back).
