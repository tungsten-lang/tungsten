## Target metric (fixed BEFORE running anything; do not change)

FALL RATE on perturbed standing starts of the trial's task (`run_v4`, 30 s), over >= 128 FRESH seeds no search ever used:
- headline seeds 12001-12128; untouched confirmation seeds 13001-13128; selection seeds for any choice between
  candidates 14001-14064 (different again).
- Counted by phase of the fall (start gear, handover, top gear, late), plus mean peak speed of the starts that stay up,
  plus the mean over ALL starts with a fall scored 0.
- PASS RULE: overall fall rate < 5 % on the 128 headline seeds AND < 7 % on the confirmation seeds, with the mean peak
  speed of the starts that stay up >= 10.0 m/s. Stretch: < 3 %.
- STOP RULE: after about six hours of compute, if the overall fall rate on the selection seeds has not dropped below
  12 %, stop and report what limits it (which phase, which mechanism).
