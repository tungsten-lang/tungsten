## Structure of the swing (first pass; fixed in the third pass)

- Three tracks on DISJOINT MUSCLE MASKS (`bat.w:1049-1052`): `hips` = `pelvis_*` + leg prefixes (`leg_prefixes`, `:510`), `torso` = `spine_*`, `arms` = both shoulders, elbows, wrists. Each track runs the same chunk list (`bat.w:664-806`: lift, set, ready, leglift, stride, plant, load, swing [arms: swing/slot, whip, release, drive], follow/arc, let_go, finish, recover; arms add regrip and close). So the "subset by body region" idea is ALREADY in the code as per-track masks; what is NOT subset is inside a track.
- Every posture is built by `posture(upper, legs, stiff)` (`bat.w:549-561`): all 16 upper joints (`BatPostures.joints`, `:158-159`) and all 11 leg joints (`leg_joints`, `:161-162`) are `Pose#set` in every chunk, with one shared `stiff`. A chunk only acts on its track's mask (`Track.new("hips", movement(:hips), track_mask(:hips))`, `:317-319`), so the effective pinned set of a keyframe is "whole mask". No keyframe leaves a joint out.
- Postures (tables `bat.w:164-245`, 16 upper + 11 leg values each): `q_set` (stance), `q_coil`/`q_load`, `q_gather` (v5 stride_load), `q_slot`, `q_mid_u`, `q_release_u`, `q_contact_u`, `q_extend_u`, `q_wrap`/`q_finish` (finish), `q_recover`; legs `legs_stance`, `legs_lift`, `legs_stride`, `legs_landed`, `legs_swing(0)`.

## The champion swing is NOT the q_slot/q_release/q_contact chunk list (verified 12:22)

Champion `bat_r2_s10.txt` sets `train 1`, `knot 1`, `through 1`, `arc 1`, `flow 1`, `lift_via 1`, `plant_via 1`, `stride_load 1`, `trunk_through 1`, `train_n 11` (params lines 2-121). With `train` on, `BatSkill#movement` calls `swing_train` (`bat.w:711`, override `bat_power.w:597-616`) and the tables `q_slot`, `q_release_u`, `q_contact_u`, `q_extend_u` are never used for the swing. Instead each track gets 11 `TimedChunk` quintic contraction knots (`bat_power.w:608`), refilled every 20 ms while the pitch flies (`update_train`, `bat_power.w:736-829`): goal/velocity/acceleration of every program joint in the track's mask come from the blended ideal-control program (`train_state`, `:647-657`; `BatProgram.joints` = the same 16 upper joints, `bat_program.w:16`), plus `taim` aim offsets on 15 joints (`k_z_u`, `k_x_u`). The legs (hips track only) go from `legs_landed` to `legs_swing` on cubic_out (`:801-822`). So for the champion, the "keyframes" that are hand-posed are only: `set`/`ready`/`leglift`/`stride`/`plant`/`load` (pre-swing), the follow-through arc `q_wrap`/`q_finish` (`bat.w:772`), and `recover`. The swing itself is 11 dense knots x 16 joints x (q, dq, ddq) = a full-vector sampled trajectory, but with per-joint drive `dw` profile (`bat_power.w:424-432`, `dw0..dw29` = 5 groups x 6 Gaussians).

## Correction to the structure section above (third pass, 16:05): what a left-out joint does in THIS controller

The bat body is pair-per-joint (`body.w:65-78`: `geometry` is nil except for MyoSuite bodies). For such a body `Pose#apply` writes only the posed joints' pos/neg muscle pair (`contraction.w:136-144`, `set_joint` at `:60-67`), and `Chunk#goal_from` copies the pose only onto the track's own mask, "others keep their start value" (`chunk.w:216-228`). So today a joint left out of a pose HOLDS its command from the chunk start (not the reference posture; that is the MyoSuite/`apply_geometry` rule of `contraction.w:146-169`). "Hold" is expressible today by omission; "interpolate between the neighbours that pin it" and "release (reflex only)" are not (release would need a gain change, `Reflex#set_gains`, bat.w:522-545).

A second structural fact that changes the question: the keyframes are already partitioned. `posture()` builds a 27-joint pose (`bat.w:549-561`), but each of the three tracks executes only its mask slice: hips = `pelvis_*` + legs (12 joints: pelvis_yaw, pelvis_shift, 10 leg joints), torso = `spine_*` (3), arms = 12 (`bat.w:1049-1052`). So "subset by body region" is in the code; the open questions are inside a track.

## 1. Stance: `lift` / `set` / `ready` (all three pose `q_set` + `legs_stance`)

- **Definition.** `bat.w:670-673` (`lift`, `set`, `ready`); `p_set = posture(q_set, stance, @stiff)` `:631`, `p_ready` = same pose at `@stiff * 1.5` `:632`; tables `q_set` `:164-165`, `legs_stance` `:229-230`. Champion (`params/bat_r2_s10.txt`): stiff 0.0553 (l.89), lift_at 0.1113 (l.76), step_delay 0.1806 (l.88). q_set values (pelvis_yaw, spine flex/lat/rot): 0.05, 0.20, 0.00, 0.30; left arm sh flex/abd/rot, elbow, wrist flex/dev: -0.082, -0.184, 0.559, 2.106, -0.460, -0.127; right arm: -0.473, -0.642, 0.290, 1.979, -0.693, -0.200.
- **Intent.** The batter's stance with both hands on the bat. The arm values are an IK solution, not a hand-picked angle set: "upper-body postures keep the top hand on the handle wherever both hands hold the bat (grip closure does not depend on the trunk, so a posture's arms can ride any trunk)" (`bat.w:150-153`). So what is constrained is "bat at pose X, both hands gripped", a task-space constraint that no per-joint subset can state.
- **Evidence.** None on individual stance joints (no ablation found in `scratchpad/bat/LOG.md`, README 4134-4450: unverified). Structural: `lift` and `set` pose the identical `p_set`; `lift` exists as the first-chunk carrier of `correct(idle, @spring)` and was "kept so the practised runs stay bit-identical" (`bat.w:666-670`), and the movement loops from index 1 (`mv.loop_from(1)`, `bat.w:806`), so `lift` runs once at start-up only. `ready` = `set` with stiff x1.5 (`:632`): it is a pure co-contraction keyframe (same angles), held until `delivery(lift_at + step_delay)` (`:673`).
- **Proposed subset.** None for the angles: the full vector is the IK solution that keeps the grip. `lift` could be dropped (same pose as `set`). The interesting subset is stiffness: `ready`'s x1.5 co-contraction is applied to all 27 joints; only the arms/torso (bat holding, trunk stability) plausibly need it. No per-joint stiffness experiment was found (unverified).
- **Today.** Drop `lift`: yes, but replays stop being bit-identical (comment at `:666-669`). Per-joint stiffness: yes, `Pose#set(joint, q, stiff)` already takes one per joint (`contraction.w:113`).
- **Estimate.** Value 1, cost 1, risk 2 (drop `lift`); stiffness-per-group experiment value 2, cost 2, risk 2.

## 2. `leglift` (`p_lift` = `q_coil` + `legs_lift`)

- **Definition.** `bat.w:674-681` (`TimedChunk "leglift"`, `.via`, retimed by `retime_lifts` to end when the stride is due, `:676-679`, `:854`); `q_lift = q_coil` (`:621, :625`); tables `q_coil` `:170-171`, `legs_lift` `:232-233`. Champion: dur_leglift 0.2465 (params l.24), lift_via 1 (l.77), lift_at 0.1113, stride_at 0.4711 (l.90).
- **Intent.** The front (right) leg lifts and the trunk coils before the stride. The front foot is `r_foot` (`planted = Latch.contact("r_foot", 0.02)`, `bat.w:685`).
- **What it really changes relative to `set`/`ready` (computed from the tables, `bat.w:164-171, 229-233`).** Upper body: pelvis_yaw +0.15, spine_lat +0.05, spine_rot +0.25 (0.30 -> 0.55); all 12 arm joints and spine_flex are IDENTICAL to `q_set` (the `q_coil` arm entries are copied digit for digit). Legs: only the five front-leg joints move (r_hip_flex +0.58, r_hip_abd -0.47, r_hip_rot +0.52, r_knee +0.98, r_ankle +0.83); pelvis_shift and all five back-leg (l_*) joints are within 0.05 rad of `legs_stance`. So this 27-joint keyframe is an 8-joint subset (3 trunk-rotation joints + 5 front-leg joints) padded with 19 unchanged values.
- **Evidence.** `dur_leglift` 0.2 / 0.3: "NO EFFECT AT ALL (bit-identical)" (`scratchpad/bat/LOG.md:335`, README 4405-4406), because `lift_via` retimes the chunk. `lift_at` changes only the flow score (LOG 335, 337: 0.09 -> flow 2.379, 0.14 -> 2.292 with speed unchanged). The README pins the remaining hitch on the lift: "a lift -> plant dip of 0.58-0.61 (the leg lift's apex) remains" (README 4389; S10 moved it 0.607 -> 0.579). No ablation of individual lift joints (unverified which of the five front-leg joints sets the apex; the dip is the measured contraction speed, not a joint).
- **Proposed subset.** Pinned: front leg (r_hip_flex, r_knee for the apex; r_hip_abd/rot and r_ankle as IK companions), pelvis_yaw and spine_rot (the coil). Back leg, pelvis_shift, spine_flex, spine_lat and all 12 arm joints: HOLD (their keyframe value already equals the stance value, so "hold" is exactly what happens today, apart from stiffness).
- **Today.** Yes for hold: on the arms track, `leglift` can be a chunk with no `.pose()` (`@goal_kind = :hold`, `chunk.w:66`), and on the pair-per-joint body an omitted joint keeps its start command (`chunk.w:216-228`). One caveat, stiffness: `ready` runs at `@stiff * 1.5` (`bat.w:632`) and `leglift` at `@stiff` (`:674`), so dropping the arms from `leglift` would keep the arms at x1.5 instead of easing to x1.0. That is a behaviour change, so it is not bit-identical.
- **Estimate.** Value 1, cost 1, risk 2. Nothing indicates a speed gain; the benefit is clarity and a cheaper pose table. The flow score (lift valley) is the only thing the lift moves and it moves with timing (`lift_at`, `drv_stride`), not with the pose values.

## 3. `stride` (`q_gather` + `legs_stride`, leg burst)

- **Definition.** `bat.w:682-684`: `p_stride = posture(q_load, legs_stride, stiff)` with `q_load = gathered = q_gather` for the champion (`stride_load 1`, `gather` left at its default 1.0, `bat.w:469, :626, :892-896`); `drive(burst(lj, legs_lift, legs_stride, mask, drv_stride, 0.15))` (`:683`); `st.via` when `plant_via` (`:690`). Champion: dur_stride 0.3270 (params l.28), drv_stride 0.6491 (l.16), stride_at 0.4711, step_delay 0.1806. Tables `q_gather` `:203-207`, `legs_stride` `:235-236`.
- **Intent.** The front foot steps toward the pitcher while the hands load back and the front shoulder closes further ("the front shoulder closed further (pelvis 0.25, spine rotation 0.68) and the top hand 8 cm back toward the catcher and 3 cm up, the bat's angle kept, grip closed", `bat.w:203-205`).
- **What it changes relative to `q_coil`.** Trunk: spine_rot +0.13 (0.55 -> 0.68), pelvis_yaw +0.05. Arms: l_sh_flex +0.23, l_sh_rot -0.14, l_elbow -0.10, r_sh_flex +0.22, r_sh_rot +0.09, and the bottom wrist r_wrist_flex +0.58 and r_wrist_dev +0.34; abductions, l_wrist_*, r_elbow unchanged. The two big wrist moves are IK compensation to keep "the bat's angle" while the hands go back (`bat.w:205`): along for the ride, task-space (bat angle) constrained, not joint constrained. Legs (hips track, from `legs_lift`): the stride moves both legs (front r_hip_abd +0.73, r_knee -0.60; back l_hip_rot -0.44, l_ankle +0.34; pelvis_shift +0.18: the weight shift), so the leg part is a full-leg keyframe.
- **Evidence.** Timing matters, posture values untested: `dur_stride` 0.28 -> 15/16 hits with an aim shift, 0.38 -> 6/16 hits; `stride_at` 0.55 -> 4/16 hits, 0.40 -> +0.20 m/s with flow +0.14 and aim shifted; `step_delay` 0.195 -> 12/16 hits (`LOG.md:339-340`). `drv_stride` 0.3 -> flow 2.405, 0.8 -> speed 26.550 and flow 2.305 (-0.04) against the base 26.524 / 2.348 (`LOG.md:334, :337`); the champion moved it 0.53 -> 0.65 (LOG 352). `gather` was never searched or scanned (absent from the S1, S2, S10 tunable lists, `LOG.md:81, :333-343`: unverified effect).
- **Proposed subset.** Legs: pinned = front-foot landing placement (r_hip_flex/abd/rot, r_knee, r_ankle end values) plus pelvis_shift; the back leg moves only to keep the foot planted under the turning hips (derived, could follow by IK). Trunk: pin spine_rot and pelvis_yaw (hip-shoulder separation is the stated purpose, `bat.w:1054-1056`). Arms: pin "hands back 8 cm" as a pair (l_sh_flex, r_sh_flex, l_sh_rot), hold the rest; the two wrist deflections are compensation and could be left to the reflex layer or to an IK solve (not expressible as a joint subset).
- **Today.** Only as a constraint-by-omission on the arms track (hold the wrists: they would then keep their `set` values and the bat angle would drift by about the solved 0.58 / 0.34 rad, which is exactly what the IK removes). So the arms part needs the full vector; a subset would need a task-space (bat pose) pin, which the SDK does not have.
- **Estimate.** Legs subset: value 2, cost 3, risk 4 (timing is already tight: a few ms of `step_delay` flips hit counts). Arms subset: value 1, cost 4, risk 4. Test that costs nothing: scan the existing `gather` tunable (0.1-1.0) to see whether the full hand-load is needed at all.

## 4. `plant` (`q_gather` + `legs_landed`; a via keyframe)

- **Definition.** `bat.w:685-693`: `Chunk.new("plant", @dur_plant).pose(p_load).hold_until(planted, @dur_plant).via`, `planted = Latch.contact("r_foot", 0.02)`; `p_load = posture(q_load, legs_load, stiff)` with `legs_load = legs_landed` (`:621-622, :633`); table `legs_landed` `:238-239`. Champion: dur_plant 0.1043 (params l.26), plant_via 1 (l.81). On the arms and torso tracks the chunk also carries `correct(pre_aim, @spring_pre)` (`:699-704`: the aim offsets start here).
- **Intent.** The front foot lands without a slam or a stop (`bat.w:96-98`).
- **What it changes relative to `stride`.** Upper body: NOTHING (`p_stride` and `p_load` use the same `q_load`, `:682` and `:633`); only the aim correction (an offset law on 15 joints, see section 10) rides on top. Legs, from `legs_stride` to `legs_landed`: r_hip_flex -0.47, r_hip_rot -0.61, r_knee -0.55, r_ankle -0.43; pelvis_shift, all l_* joints and r_hip_abd unchanged (within 0.05). So the chunk is a 4-joint subset (front-foot touch-down) on the hips track and a pure timing marker on the torso and arms tracks.
- **Evidence.** The front foot's contact ends the chunk (latch), so what matters is the latch time, not the pose values; `dur_plant` is only the timeout and was not scanned (absent from the S10 leg scan, `LOG.md:333-341`: unverified). `plant` ends at the first of the foot contact latch and the `dur_plant` timeout (`chunk.w:102-105`, `controller.w:209-211`); after it, the hips fire on average 17-18 ms after the plant (README 4378-4384, 4386-4389), so the `load` that follows lasts about 17-18 ms and the plant-to-load handoff is nearly a single event.
- **Proposed subset.** Hips track: pin the four front-foot joints (r_hip_flex, r_hip_rot, r_knee, r_ankle); the rest hold. Torso and arms tracks: no pose at all, only the latch (a hold chunk).
- **Today.** Yes: a hold chunk on the two upper-body tracks, and on the hips track a pose that leaves the l_* joints and pelvis_shift out (they keep their start command, `chunk.w:216-228`). Stiffness is not an issue here (`stride` and `plant` both use `@stiff`, `bat.w:682, :691`), so the upper-body result should be bit-identical; the hips result is identical only if the start command of the omitted joints equals the `legs_stride` values (true at the end of `stride` within the chunk's own tracking error: unverified).
- **Estimate.** Value 1, cost 1, risk 1. Purely structural.

## 5. `load` (`q_gather` + `legs_landed`; TimedChunk via)

- **Definition.** `bat.w:698, 826-834` (`load_chunk`: `TimedChunk.new("load", dur).pose(p_load).hold_until(due, 1.5).via`, retimed every tick by `retime_loads`, `:842-849`, to the time until the swing is due, clamped to [`load_min` 0.04, `load_max` 0.6] `:346-347`). Same pose as `plant` (`hip_open` is 0 so `p_ld = p_load`, `:697`). Champion: flow 1, timed_load follows `flow` (`:341-344`); lag_arms 0.0176, lag_torso 0.0032 (params l.70-71); prog_trig 0.2478 (l.82).
- **Intent.** Hold the loaded posture until the swing fires, but still moving (a small counter-movement) when the swing starts (`bat.w:820-825`).
- **Evidence.** In the champion the load is almost gone: plant -> hips fire is 17-18 ms (README 4342, 4384), stall before the swing 8 ms. The 11 swing knots of every track read the PROGRAM's absolute angles (`train_state`, `bat_power.w:647-657`), not the load pose, so the load's only effect is the state the command and the joints are in at the fire. The program library was captured from the v5 swing's state at the hips firing (README 4158-4162, `ceil_sweet.sh` in LOG 300-302: "captured 20 ms before the hips fire"), and v5's load is `q_gather` (`bat.w:94-96`). So `q_gather` is pinned by provenance (the start state the 6 programs were optimised from), not by a measured sensitivity. No ablation of individual load joints exists (unverified).
- **Proposed subset.** None that the evidence supports for the values. `plant` and `load` are the same pose and could be fused into one keyframe whose end is "foot down AND swing due" (a latch); the via-chain `stride -> plant -> load` passes the identical control point three times.
- **Today.** Fusing: yes (`Latch.all` exists, `latch.w:45`; `Latch.any` is what `bat.w:651, :675` use), but the scores are timing-critical (section 3), so any change needs a full champion re-evaluation.
- **Estimate.** Value 1, cost 2, risk 3. Drop as dead structure only if a bit-identical replay is acceptable to lose.

## 6. The swing itself in the champion: 11 quintic knots per track (`train 1`), i.e. there is no contact keyframe

- **Definition.** `bat_power.w:597-616` (`swing_train`: `TimedChunk ... .to(zeros).knot(zeros, zeros).via.drive(zeros).gain(g)` x `train_n`), refilled every 20 ms until the hips fire by `update_train` (`:736-829`): per knot and per program joint in the track's mask, goal, velocity and acceleration from `train_state` (`:647-657`, the blend of 6 ideal-control programs), plus the `taim` offset (section 10) and a drive term. The program joints are the 16 upper-body joints (`bat_program.w:16`). Champion values (`params/bat_r2_s10.txt`): train_n 11 (l.113), train_end -0.0271 (l.106, last knot 27 ms AFTER the program's contact), train_lead 0.0171 (l.110), train_dlead 0.00076 (l.104), train_first 1.146 (l.108), train_ext 0.0095 (l.107), train_drv 1.115 (l.105), train_gain 0.848 (l.109), dshape_b 0.465 (l.19), dw0-dw29 (l.31-60), prog_trig 0.2478 (l.82).

- **Evidence about which joints matter for bat speed.** (1) Strength sweep with the fixed controller (README 4437-4447; LOG 195-204): pelvis yaw x2 +0.46 m/s; trunk (pelvis yaw + spine) x1.5 +1.28; elbows x2 +1.21; arms (shoulders, elbows, wrists) x1.5 +2.0; "the arms are a bigger lever than the trunk (the wrist whip carries the speed)". (2) The CMA ceiling's pattern: "the pelvis turns at full activation into its yaw limit, the spine stays coiled, the top arm's elbow extends, the lead shoulder abducts and rotates, and the bottom wrist, held at full radial deviation, whips through 0.8 rad of ulnar deviation in the last 60 ms" (README 4162-4167). (3) Kinematics: bottom wrist 1065 deg/s (1.77x its unloaded limit: "the arms fling it"), bottom elbow 0.70 and top elbow 0.49 of their limits, pelvis yaw 0.32, none but the wrists near a range end (README 4414-4419; LOG 147-152). (4) Drive timing per group (pelvis, spine, shoulders, elbows, wrists; `bat_power.w:416-432`): a time profile gave +0.42 m/s on all 48 pitches, "the pelvis' factor rises to 1.5 at 150 ms, the spine's and shoulders' too, the last 40 ms about 15 % less" (README 4399-4403). ## Other observations for the SDK answer

- Two "left out" semantics coexist: `Pose` (hold the start command on a pair-per-joint body, reference posture on a MyoSuite body, `contraction.w:136-169`) and `Chunk#to` (0 for every unlisted muscle of the mask, `chunk.w:223-224`). A subset-keyframe API should say which it means per call.
- The three disjoint track masks (`bat.w:1049-1052`) already realise "subset by region" for free; a per-track pose slice is what `goal_from` does (`chunk.w:225-227`). What the SDK cannot say is a task-space pin (bat pose, feet planted, hands on the bat), which is what the stance, stride and legs keyframes really are (`bat.w:150-153, :203-205`); they are IK solutions stored as full joint vectors.
- Run-time keyframing is first class here: `update_train` rewrites `to()`, `knot()`, `drive()` and `retime()` of live chunks every 20 ms (`bat_power.w:823-828`), which is the natural place for a "hold / extrapolate / release" per-joint policy.
- Not verified: whether the athlete body has the biarticular leg muscles (`l_rf`, `l_ham`, `l_gas`) that `leg_prefixes` lists "on the human-calibrated body" (`bat.w:508-511`); for such muscles a joint left out of a pose would interact with the neighbouring joint's command.
