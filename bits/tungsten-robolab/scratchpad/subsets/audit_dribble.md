## 0. Structure: the dribble family is already built from per-part tracks (a subset mechanism)

Every dribble controller here runs four parallel `Track`s, each owning a disjoint muscle mask, so a "keyframe" is a chunk of ONE track and poses only that part's joints:

- `stance` track, mask = every muscle not owned by another track (`rest_of(used)`, `dribble_v3.w:508-513`, added at `dribble_v3.w:341` and `dribble_btl.w:591`). Its pose (`stance_pose`, `dribble_v3.w:643-654`; BTL variant `dribble_btl.w:682-694`) sets 10 leg joints (hip flex, abd, rot, knee, ankle, both sides) plus `pelvis_yaw`, `spine_lat`, `spine_rot`.
- `trunk` track, mask `spine_flex` (plus `spine_rot` when `rotating?`, `dribble_btl.w:581-582`). Pose `spine_pose` (`dribble_v3.w:689-692`) sets `spine_flex` only; the rotating variant `twist_pose` (`dribble_btl.w:639-642`) sets `spine_flex` and `spine_rot`.
- `right` / `left` tracks, mask = that arm's shoulder, elbow, wrist and finger muscles (`dribble_btl.w:579-580, 593-594`). Pose `hand_pose` (`dribble_v3.w:737-746`) sets the 6 arm joints, plus the two finger joints when the body has fingers.

So the legs are never posed inside an arm chunk and the arms are never posed inside a leg chunk: the arm-only and leg-only subsets the brief asks about already exist at TRACK level. What does not exist is a subset INSIDE a track (for example, a hand chunk that pins only shoulder and wrist and lets the elbow float), and the stance track always poses all 10 leg joints plus 3 trunk joints in every chunk, even though only the lead foot and back foot placement is intended (see section 2).

Reflex gains are also set per mask (`dribble_btl.w:583-586`: arms `arm_kp` 12.13 / `arm_kd` 0.611, fingers `f_kp` 2.48, legs `leg_kp/leg_kd`).

## 1. BTL champion: what actually moves (chunk census, `dribble_btl_*` champions)

Champion values come from `scratchpad/btl2/params/pgP0LRz2.txt` (no `bt_rotl`/`bt_rotr`, no `bt_plan`, no `bt_rplan`, no `st_stepin`: all defaults of 0). The program tables `pg*` are 4119 of its lines (`grep -c '^pg'`); `mode 6`, `pg_on 1`, `pg_first 0`, `pg_n 3`, `pg_stages 3`, `pg_nz 3`, `pg_gain 0` (`pgP0LRz2.txt`).

Census of what the champion really runs (read from the code, not from the chunk names):

- **Stance track: ONE held pose.** With `st_stepin` 0 the stance track jumps once to chunk `l_lead` and holds it for ever (`dribble_btl.w:599-604`, chunk `hold_until(never)` at `dribble_v3.w:671`). BTL `choose` has no stance logic (`dribble_btl.w:851-870`), so the 24 other stance chunks of the 25 built (`athletic`, all lift/place/lead-change chunks, `dribble_v3.w:662-685`) are never entered. They are dead code in this champion; `st_step`, `st_lhip`, `st_lknee` are unread in practice (`st_stepin` is 0) and `st_hold` only times the launch's `hold` (`dribble_btl.w:713`).
- **Trunk track: ONE held pose.** `hold` (spine_flex 0.55) is never left: `rotating?` is false (`dribble_btl.w:493-494, 648`) and V3's `entering` jumps to `b_up`/`up` only when `zh == b_zh` (0.838 default, `dribble_v3.w:587-589`), which no BTL chunk passes (BTL passes `bt_zh` 0.790 / `bl_zh` 0.783 / `z_hit`). `b_up`, `up` are unused.
- **Arm tracks: the only time-varying parts.** Right hand: `hold`, `lift`, `throw`, `let_go` (the launch, `dribble_btl.w:768-786`), then `x_recover` / `x_wait` / `x_swing` / `x_carry` / `idle` (`:799-809`) and, for passes 0 to 2, `g_run` (the muscle program, `:847-848`). Left hand: `rest` then the same cycle. `recover`/`wait`/`swing`/`carry` (`:790-793`, plain dribble) and the `y_*` chain are entered only for `move == "D"` or `bt_ychain`, neither of which occurs with script "B" (`script_for(6)`, `dribble_btl.w:513-519`) and `bt_ychain` 0.

So the question "which joints does each keyframe constrain" reduces to four things: the held lunge (section 2), the held trunk (section 3), the launch (section 4), and the pass stroke plus its three muscle programs (sections 5 and 6).

## 2. BTL lunge (`stance` track, chunk `l_lead`)

- **Definition.** `stance_pose` BTL variant `dribble_btl.w:682-694`: 10 leg joints (hip flex, abd, rot, knee, ankle, both legs) at `@stiff` (0.06, `dribble_v3.w:356`), `pelvis_yaw` 0, `spine_lat` 0, `spine_rot` = `@twist`. The leg angles are an IK solution (`DribbleBtlLeg#solve`, `dribble_btl.w:99-145`; `solve_stance` `:609-620`): lead ankle at (`st_fx` 0.45, `ly(st_fy)` 0.12, 0.078), back ankle at (`st_bx` -0.55, `ly(st_by)` -0.20, 0.078), flat foot (two soft orientation residuals, weight 0.3), pelvis height from the task. Also `st_twist` 0.55 (spine rotation, lead-side shoulder back) and `spine` 0.55 (section 3). Champion: `pgP0LRz2.txt` st_bx -0.55, st_by -0.2, st_fx 0.45, st_fy 0.12, st_twist 0.55 (round numbers from a planner, not CMA: "the cycle planner's reachable back-and-forth (lead foot 0.45,0.12; back foot -0.55,-0.2; trunk twist 0.55; right spot 0.45,-0.3; left spot -0.1,0.35)", `params/dribble_btl_a2.txt:1`, unchanged in A3, A4, c2 and the ORACLE champion (`grep` of `params/dribble_btl_*.txt`); the code defaults are 0.35 / 0.17 / -0.4 / -0.24, `dribble_btl.w:159-162`; the earlier Q2 geometry was a lunge at pelvis 0.72 with feet (0.40, 0.28) and (-0.55, -0.15), twist 0.5 to 0.58, `params/dribble_btl_q2.txt:1`). So the whole layout (feet, twist, flexion, both hand spots) is ONE planned geometry frozen since round 3; the strokes were then tuned on top of it, which is why A4 is brittle to `spine` (`btl2/LOG.md:52`).
- **Intent.** "A staggered lunge the ball can pass under ... between the front shin and the back knee" (`dribble_btl.w:6-11`). The task counts a pass only if the ball crosses the leg gate below the crotch and touches no other body part (`trials.json:1352`).
- **Evidence.**
  - The planners score a layout by leg-axis polylines (hip, knee, ankle xy) for the gate plus capsule clearance of thigh and shin against the ball's path, then hand reach (`scratchpad/btl/plan7.py:1-5, 24-40`). So what the legs are for is the two leg axes and their capsules, nothing about the individual hip abduction, hip rotation or ankle angles.
  - The IK uses all 5 joints to place 3 ankle coordinates plus 2 soft flat-foot residuals, regularised 0.001 toward a seed (`dribble_btl.w:95-145`): no redundancy is left, foot yaw (hip rotation) is the only free direction and the solver simply leaves it near its seed (0.0 / 0.05, `:615-616`).
  - Leg contacts are real failure modes of weak passes: "some releases bounce 0.3-0.4 m too far out (0.02, 0.4) and hit the lead leg" (`btl2/LOG.md:117-118`), "4 leg hits" (`:138`), pass 2 'body' 11 of 30 failures in one chain (`:146`).
  - No leg-joint ablation found (unverified whether any leg joint was ever varied on its own): the stance layout was planned, not searched by CMA (`btl2/LOG.md:50-51`; `README.md:4505-4512`).
- **Proposed subset.** Already a task-space subset: pinned = the two ankle xy positions (and thigh/knee height over the gate); free = foot yaw and the individual hip abduction/rotation. The IK already treats them that way. Do NOT release the legs to their start command: the stance mask is `rest_of(used)` (every muscle outside the arms and `spine_flex`, `dribble_btl.w:587-591`), so an unposed leg joint keeps its measured start command, and the body stands on these legs.
- **Today.** Yes by omission of `p.set` lines in `stance_pose`, but nothing is gained: per `contraction.w:149-169` a muscle crossing a posed and an unposed joint is evaluated with the unposed joint at the reference posture, and a muscle crossing only unposed joints keeps the start command (`controller.w:642-650`, `Track` goals `controller.w:60`).
- **Estimate.** Value 1, cost 2, risk 3. A full joint vector is right here because it is an IK solution of a task-space subset.

## 3. BTL trunk (`trunk` track, chunk `hold`: `spine_flex` 0.55; `spine_rot` and `spine_lat`, `pelvis_yaw` inside the stance pose)

- **Definition.** `trunk_movement` (V3) `dribble_v3.w:697-703`: `hold` = `spine_pose(@spine)` (only `spine_flex`, stiffness `t_stiff` 0.0 so no co-contraction, `:689-692`), champion `spine 0.55`. `spine_rot` = `st_twist` 0.55 is posed by the stance track (`dribble_btl.w:693`); `pelvis_yaw` and `spine_lat` are posed at 0 (`:691-692`). The arm IK assumes exactly this base: `pose_base(spine)` = pelvis xyz, yaw 0, flexion `spine`, lat 0, twist `@twist` (`dribble_btl.w:664-667`).
- **Intent.** Lean and twist the trunk so both shoulders reach the ball at the two hand spots (`dribble_btl.w:169-172`).
- **Evidence.**
  - Pinned by evidence (strongly): "A4 is brittle: changing spine 0.55 -> 0.5 loses pass 1 outright (its arrival point moves 0.4 m)" (`btl2/LOG.md:52`).
  - Twist is what makes the back-left spot reachable at all: with the twist (+0.55) the left palm reaches (-0.15..0.35, 0.35, 0.85..1.05) with 0-6 cm error at flexion 0.55 and under 2 cm at flexion 0.2-0.35; without twist x < 0.05 is out of reach (`btl2/LOG.md:32-35`).
  - Changing the twist during play hurts: any nonzero `bt_rotl` loses (ok1 12 -> 6, ok2 4 -> 0) "the arm IK uses the commanded, not the measured, twist" (`btl2/LOG.md:53-54`).
  - `pelvis_yaw` and `spine_lat`: no evidence either way; they are pinned only because the arm model has them as fixed zeros (unverified whether their muscles are even near-free on this body).
- **Proposed subset.** Nothing to free: flexion and twist are both pinned (they are inputs of every arm pose solve and the evidence says the pass chain is brittle to them). `pelvis_yaw`/`spine_lat` could float only if the arm IK were given their measured values, which it is not.
- **Today.** n/a. The trunk is already a separate one-chunk track with only the flexion, plus a twist owned by the stance track (an awkward split, since `rotating?` moves `spine_rot` into the trunk track, `dribble_btl.w:581-582`).
- **Estimate.** Value 1, cost 1, risk 4. Do not touch.

## 4. BTL launch (right hand, chunks `hold` / `lift` / `throw` / `let_go`)

- **Definition.** `dribble_btl.w:768-786`. `hold`: palm at the right spot + (`bt_lhx`, `bt_lhy`), height pelvis z + 0.19, level (pitch 0, roll 0; `:768`), timed by `st_hold` (default 0.8 s, `:713`; champion has no `st_hold`). `lift`: palm at `bt_lzc` over the spot, level (`:769, 779`, `t_lift` 0.4 s default). `throw` / `let_go`: palm down to `bt_lzl` at (`bt_ldx`, `bt_ldy`), normal straight down (`:770, 785-786`), burst scaled by `bt_ldrv`, grip opens when the palm is below `z_rel` (`let_go`, `dribble_v3.w:601-603`). Every pose is `hand_pose` = 6 arm joints plus 2 finger joints (`dribble_v3.w:737-746`). Champion (also `scratchpad/btl/launch_search9.log`, last lines): bt_lhx 0.0721, bt_lhy -0.0291, bt_ldx 0.1029, bt_ldy -0.0026, bt_lt 0.0895, bt_ldrv 0.683, bt_lzc 1.164, bt_lzl 0.848, z_rel 1.033; `bt_lfix` absent = 0, so `launch_fix` returns nil (`:913`) and the launch is open-loop in hand space.
- **Intent.** Throw the ball straight down at the right spot so it rebounds to the right hand's strike zone (`dribble_btl.w:382-383`). The calibration objective was only ball state: "the ball's first rise through z=0.85 lands on the right spot, apex >= 0.95" (`scratchpad/btl/launch_search.py:1-2`), CMA over exactly the nine launch parameters above (`:9-10`).
- **Evidence.**
  - Consistent and not the limiter: release 1 has vz sd 0.12 and |vh| sd 0.13 (`btl2/LOG.md:40-41`), "A4's pass 1 is already the most repeatable stroke" (`:128-129`). What the launch does badly is its arrival spread at the left hand (y 0.23 to 0.48, `:133-134`), which the stage-0 program absorbed (`:179-180, 216-217`).
  - Joint loads are comfortable: "RIGHT arm's launch stroke is elbow extension 2.3 -> 1.4 rad at -10 rad/s (margins 0.1-0.25 rad)" (`btl2/LOG.md:46-47`), so the throw is an elbow (plus wrist) motion, not a shoulder one.
  - No joint-level ablation of the launch found (unverified).
- **Proposed subset.** Already expressed in hand space: pinned = palm xyz and the palm normal (straight down) at the release and at `bt_lzc`; the IK has 6 joints for 5 task DOF so one redundant DOF (forearm roll) is left to the regulariser (`pose_reg` 0.002, `dribble_btl.w:568, 770`). The two finger joints are posed at 0.0 (`lcock`/`llow`, `:772-773`) and never matter for a palm-down push. Nothing to gain by freeing individual joints.
- **Today.** Yes, but only by omission in `hand_pose` (the finger lines, `dribble_v3.w:743-745`), which changes nothing measurable.
- **Estimate.** Value 1, cost 1, risk 2.
- **Drop candidate.** `hold` (0.8 s wait "until the feet are set") is pure settle time when `st_stepin` is 0 (`dribble_btl.w:181-183, 599-604`): the feet never step. Unverified whether the body needs that second to reach the lunge from its initial configuration.

## 5. BTL pass-stroke poses (`bcock` = `x_recover`, `x_wait`, `idle`, `rest`; `blow` = `x_swing`, `x_carry`)

- **Definition.** `q_bc` / `q_bl` by IK, `dribble_btl.w:728-738`: the palm sits on the far top of a ball at the hand's spot, normal tilted `angc` (cock) / `angl` (low end) from straight down toward the other spot, at height `zc` / `zl`, the low end swept `sweep` along the pass; solved from an arm-posture seed `[bt_sf, bt_sa, bt_sr, bt_se]` (`:733`) that "picks the posture branch" (`:302-304`), low end regularised with `sh_reg`. Champion: right hand (back-leg side) bt_angc 0.538, bt_angl 1.353, bt_dzc 0.207, bt_dzl 0.134, bt_zh 0.790, bt_sweep 0.251 (cock height 0.997 m, low 0.656 m); left hand (lead-leg side) bl_angc 1.433, bl_angl 0.840, bl_dzc 0.054, bl_dzl 0.318, bl_zh 0.783, bl_sweep 0.232 (cock 0.838 m, low 0.466 m) (`pgP0LRz2.txt`). The posture seeds `bt_sf/sa/sr/se`, `bl_s*` are absent: defaults (sf0 0.55, sa0 0.05, rot0 -0.5, se 1.4; `dribble_v3.w:381-383`, `dribble_btl.w:312-315`).
- **Census.** Only two distinct poses exist per hand. `x_recover` (timed `t_rec`), `x_wait` (latched), `idle` (held) and the left's initial `rest` all use the same `bcock` (`dribble_btl.w:788, 799-809`); `x_swing` and `x_carry` both use `blow` and differ only in latch and correction (`:807-808`). They are states of one pose, not separate keyframes.
- **Intent.** The cocked pose waits behind the ball with the palm already above it; the low pose is the end of a downward sweep that drives the ball toward the other spot.
- **Evidence.**
  - Where the reflex stroke lives in the champion: only passes 3 and later (the left hand's second stroke, good on 9 to 14 %) plus the waiting posture before the program trigger; passes 0 to 2 are muscle programs (section 6, `dribble_btl.w:1386-1394, 1397-1428`).
  - The lead-leg (left) hand pose is at its joint limits: "shoulder_rot -1.34 of -1.40, elbow 0.3 -> 0 of limit 0, wrist_flex +1.0 of 1.2, shoulder_abd -0.1 of -0.3" (`btl2/LOG.md:44-46`); its IK cannot reach the ideal stroke for any trunk lean 0-0.55 or twist 0-0.8 (errors 4-13 cm, shoulder_rot at its limit, `:48-51`). So for the left hand a full pose is not attainable and pinning shoulder rotation, elbow and wrist flexion at values the arm cannot reach is pure fiction (cf. `audit_table_tennis.md` "calibrated fiction").
  - A4's low cock (`bl_dzc` 0.054) "is a dynamics/timing optimum", not a reach limit: a higher cock is statically reachable (`btl2/LOG.md:34-35`).
  - The idle posture is not reached when the next stroke comes: the left arm at the second stroke has wrist flexion 0.27 sd 0.445 (the pair's contraction difference) against 0.668 sd 0.067 at the first, elbow -0.52 sd 0.28: "the left arm is not settled when the second stroke comes" (`btl2/LOG.md:261-262, 266-267`); an open-loop table cannot fix that (J 12 to 16, no touch).
  - Wrist flexion, shoulder abduction and elbow are the three posture variables that explain the stroke outcome (leave-one-out R^2 for release vz 0.65, vx 0.43 with the left arm's joints against 0.24 / 0.00 with the ball alone; `README.md:4543-4546`, `btl2/LOG.md:231-233`); `--zj 4,1,3` = wrist flex, shoulder abd, elbow (`:238-239`). Shoulder flexion, shoulder rotation, wrist deviation and fingers were not offered as features (unverified that they do not matter).
- **Proposed subset (the most promising one in this area).** A "wait posture" keyframe for the tail of each program (the follow-through `idle`), pinning only wrist flexion, shoulder abduction and elbow of the stroking arm to the first-stroke values (the three measured features), holding the rest, instead of pulling all six arm joints plus the two finger joints to `bcock`. The ask in `HANDOFF_20260929.md:113` ("a tail on the left table that returns the arm to a fixed wait posture") is exactly this.
- **Today.** Expressible: a chunk after `g_run` whose pose names three joints (`Pose#set`, `contraction.w:113`) and follows on `idle`; per `contraction.w:149-169` every muscle crossing a named joint gets the contraction of the posture with unnamed joints at the reference posture (the multi-joint-crossing caveat matters only if a muscle crosses two arm joints, unverified for this body), and muscles of unnamed joints keep the START COMMAND of the chunk, `@start = ctl.command.copy` (`controller.w:58-60`). That is NOT the measured posture: during `g_run` the reflex gain is 0 (`a = drive + gain * (...)`, `reflex.w:88-91`; `g_run` `.gain(@pg_gain)` 0, `dribble_btl.w:848`) so the arm muscles get exactly the table, but the track's command still eases to `g_run`'s pose `bcock` (`controller.w:189-199`); at the end of the table the command of every arm joint is `bcock`. Omitting joints in a tail chunk would therefore keep `bcock` for them, exactly what `idle` does now. To HOLD the measured posture the tail must first copy `frame.c(i)` into `ctl.command` for the free joints (`ctl.command` is a readable, in-place writable Contraction, `controller.w:299, 647`), for instance in a `branch` hook at the end of `g_run`; unverified that such a write from skill code behaves as intended. `g_run` is followed by `idle` at `dribble_btl.w:848`. So: expressible with a small hook, not by omission alone.
- **Estimate.** Value 3, cost 2, risk 2. The wall named by the agent (release scatter; second stroke from a scattered posture) is precisely a posture-convergence problem at the end of the table.

## 6. BTL muscle programs (`g_run`: stage 0 launch strike, stage 1 left return, stage 2 right return)

- **Definition.** `g_run` chunk (`dribble_btl.w:847-848`, 0.4 s = 16 x `pg_dk` 0.025, pose `bcock`, gain `pg_gain` 0, then `idle`); the trigger `pg_trigger` starts the receiving hand's program `pg_lead` 0.165 s before the ball's predicted rising crossing of `bt_rh` and `pg_step` adds the table as drive, one value per each of 16 muscles per knot (`:1397-1472`). Table = T0 + ux Tx + uy Ty + sum of 3 posture terms Tz_i, knots formed and clamped to [0,1] (`:1448-1463`). Champion: `pg_n 3`, `pg_stages 3`, `pg_gs 1`, `pg_gmax 1`, `pg_nz 3`, `pg_nk 17`; nominal crossings pgc0 (0.520, -0.308), pgc1 (-0.125, 0.372), pgc2 (0.426, -0.270), spreads pgs 0.02-0.07 (`pgP0LRz2.txt`). Muscle order: for joints [shoulder flex, abd, rot, elbow, wrist flex, dev, finger mcp, pip] the pos then the neg muscle (`dribble_btl.w:559-562`).
- **Intent.** An open-loop activation table found by CMA on a bit-exact replay (`btl2/LOG.md:74-80`), so the stroke delivers the full ball energy that the reflex stroke cannot (`README.md:4514-4519`).
- **What the pose does here.** Nothing: `activation = drive + gain * (...)` with gain 0 (`reflex.w:88-91`), "the arm muscles get EXACTLY the table (max 2e-5)" (`btl2/LOG.md:199`). The table is already a muscle-level subset: 16 arm muscles of one hand, nothing else. Everything outside them (other arm, legs, trunk) keeps running its own tracks and reflexes.
- **Evidence on which arm joints matter (mine, read-only arithmetic on `pgP0LRz2.txt`: T0 clipped to [0,1] at the nominal crossing, mean over the 17 knots of |pos - neg| per joint).** Every one of the eight joint groups is used by every stage; none is silent. Stage 0 (launch strike): shoulder flex 0.36, abd 0.54, rot 0.30, elbow 0.46, wrist flex 0.45, wrist dev 0.34, finger mcp 0.22, pip 0.14. Stage 1 (left return): 0.51, 0.60, 0.49, 0.43, 0.43, 0.46, 0.43, 0.35. Stage 2 (right return): 0.34, 0.50, 0.53, 0.45, 0.51, 0.45, 0.45, 0.35. Weakest: finger pip and mcp in stage 0 (0.14, 0.22). The tables were not sparsity-regularised, so a nonzero value does not prove the joint matters; no per-joint ablation of a table is recorded (unverified). Raw T0 values stray outside [0,1] by design (the converter writes T0 unclipped for gain-scheduled tables, `btl2/LOG.md:161-162`), so the raw means are not activations.
- **Evidence from the gain blocks (same arithmetic: largest |gain| over the knots, per joint group, pos and neg muscles together).** The steering is spread over all groups, not concentrated in a subset. Stage 0 (launch strike) is nearly open-loop: every block is small (ball-position gains 0.12 to 0.29; it has no posture blocks, the file holds `pgz` tables for stages 1 and 2 only). Stage 1 (left return): position gains up to 0.84 (elbow, along x), 0.62 (shoulder rotation and wrist deviation, along x), 0.56 (wrist flexion, along y), posture blocks 0.11 to 0.23. Stage 2 (right return): 0.74 (finger mcp, along x), 0.75 (wrist flexion, along y), 0.68 (shoulder flexion, along y), posture blocks 0.18 to 0.34 (largest shoulder abduction 0.33 and finger pip 0.34 in the abduction-feature block). The fingers carry some of the largest position gains of stage 2 although the fingered body "has no measurable effect" elsewhere (section 13): a sign of unregularised search, not evidence that the fingers matter (unverified).
- **Evidence on the mechanism.** Seed 716's left return: cock at palm z 0.89, a small lift to 0.95 with wrist flexion to +1.26 (the limit), then a downswing starting 0.15 s before contact, palm 3.3 m/s at contact (A4: 1.9), a 95 ms carry that keeps accelerating the ball: wrist flexion +1.26 -> -0.58, elbow 0.68 -> -0.07, shoulder abduction 0.46 -> 0.08, shoulder flexion -0.03 -> 0.36 (`btl2/LOG.md:85-87`). The moving joints are wrist flexion, elbow, shoulder abduction and shoulder flexion; shoulder rotation and wrist deviation are not listed as moving there (unverified whether they stay).

## 10. Verdict and summary

Honest answer for this area: the full-vector keyframe is essentially right, because the dribble family is already built from per-part tracks on disjoint muscle masks (section 0) and its arm poses are hand-space IK poses, i.e. already task-space subsets (palm point and normal pinned, one redundant DOF left to the regulariser). There is no runner-style "toe-off" chunk where most of the joints are irrelevant. What subset thinking adds is at three places (rows 5, 6 and 7 of the table; the rest are "leave as is", with the evidence):

| # | element | subset proposal | value | cost | risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 | end of each muscle program (follow-through to `idle`) | wait-posture tail pinning wrist flexion, shoulder abduction, elbow; hold the other arm joints at their measured state, not at `bcock` | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| 6 | the three 16-muscle tables | per-joint ablation on the bit-exact replay to drop joint groups from the fit (fingers? wrist deviation? shoulder rotation?) | 2 | 2 | 1 |
| 7 | round-1 trunk pump, left guard | test `pump` 0; release the left guard to a measured hold | 2 / 1 | 1 / 1 | 2 / 2 |
| 2 | BTL lunge | none (IK from foot xy is already the subset) | 1 | 2 | 3 |
| 3 | BTL trunk flexion and twist | none: pinned by evidence (`spine` 0.55 to 0.5 loses pass 1) | 1 | 1 | 4 |
| 4 | BTL launch | none; `hold` wait maybe droppable | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| 8 | crossover champions | hold wrist deviation, spine lat/rot; foreign frozen controller | 1 | 3 | 4 |

The question "legs and trunk just need to stay out of the way" has a measured answer in the BTL champion: they do not. The closed-loop ball differs from the arm-only replay by 4 cm in arrival and 0.25 m/s in release because "the trunk/legs/other arm react to the new stroke" (`btl2/LOG.md:199-203`; `README.md:4537-4542`), and the pass chain is brittle to the trunk angle (`:52`). Freeing the rest of the body would worsen that coupling; the refits that worked kept it pinned and in the loop.

## 11. Cheapest later A/B (top candidate from this family)

Replay-only, no controller change: with the recorded chains (`scratchpad/btl2/runs/rec_s0v4.pkl`, `rec_s1v5.pkl`, `rec_m1v3.pkl` are kept for this purpose, `btl2/LOG.md:281`) and `oracle_lib.py`'s 0.03 s replay (`btl2/LOG.md:75-77`), ablate one joint group at a time in a champion table (pos/neg pair replaced by the reflex-held value) and read the replay J on the 80 training and 20 validation starts. A joint whose removal moves J by less than the replay-vs-closed-loop gap (0.037 vs 0.057 after ILC, `README.md:4541`) can be dropped from the fit; if the joint's ablation is free for all three stages, only then is a "free joint" tail worth building. Closed-loop follow-up on the dev starts 901-932 (15 s, `chain.py`, baseline streak mean 2.125, `btl2/LOG.md:269`): the wait-posture tail of section 5 on the left hand's second stroke (pass index 3, today 9 to 14 % good). Expect at best a small gain: the agent's own final experiments found the second stroke limited by a posture scatter of wrist flexion (sd 0.445), which is exactly what a tail would address.

## 12. Notes for the SDK answer

- Track-level subsets already exist and are the workhorse here (`Track.new(name, movement, mask)`, `controller.w:10-14`, `dribble_v3.w:341-344`): arm-only and leg-only keyframes are just chunks of different tracks.
- A joint left out of a pose keeps the chunk's START COMMAND (`controller.w:58-60`), which after a reflex-gain-0 chunk is the goal of that chunk's pose, not the measured posture. A "hold measured" subset therefore needs an explicit capture, e.g. a `Pose#keep(joint)` that copies `frame.c(i)` into the start vector at chunk entry (`enter_chunk` runs `goal_from` BEFORE the `on_enter` callback, `controller.w:58-67`, so a callback write comes too late for the same chunk; a `branch` hook on the previous chunk is called at its end, before the next chunk is entered, `chunk.w:154-161`, `controller.w:128-130`; that a write into `ctl.command` from skill code then takes effect is untested, unverified).
- A chunk with gain 0 ignores its pose entirely (`reflex.w:88-91`); programs written as `drive` tables (`ctl.add_drive`, `dribble_btl.w:1464`) are muscle-level subsets with no pose semantics at all.

## 13. Command minus reached, and the fingers

- **The hand-space keyframes are calibrated around a command-versus-reached gap**, as in the table-tennis audit, so omitting joints would need re-practice, not just edits. Code says so in four places: "aiming past the measured miss, since the arm lags its command" (`dribble.w:72-73`); "the arm droops and lags its model, so the aim is practised" (`dribble_btl.w:389-390`, the launch aim `bt_lhx/lhy/ldx/ldy` are practised offsets, champion 0.072 / -0.029 / 0.103 / -0.003); "the palm planned `bt_rin` inside the ball's surface (the arm lags its plan; the contact must happen)" (`dribble_btl.w:266-267`); "the arm IK uses the commanded, not the measured, twist" (`btl2/LOG.md:54`). The feedback gain that aims past the measured miss is large: `hand_fb` 0.964 in the BTL champion (`pgP0LRz2.txt`), 0.777 in round 1 (`params/dribble.txt:12`), 0.476 in the crossover champion (`params/codex/dribble_crossover_25_r9.txt`); the mechanism is the term `target + hand_fb * (target - measured palm)` in `hand_fix` (`dribble_v3.w:995-996`, `dribble.w:885-886`; `meet_fix` and `launch_fix` carry the same term but are off in the BTL champion, `dribble_btl.w:893-894, 924-925`, `bt_dzup` and `bt_lfix` absent). The claim "arms lag the plan by 5-10 cm" (line 387 of the abilities page draft) is flagged unsourced by the fact-check (`scratchpad/abilities/factcheck_report.md:249`), so no measured gap size is on file (unverified).
- **Fingers.** Only the v3 (fingered) body has them (`dribble_v3.w:316`, `dribble_btl.w:565`); the round-1 champion is "v1 body" (`champions.json:1542`) and the Codex crossovers run on the v1 body with rigid paddle hands (`factcheck_report.md:253-255`). Evidence for the joints: "Fingers built, no measurable effect": the fingered crossover lost 4.28 against 3.72 (p 0.145), Codex's own diagnostic calls it "an untrained integration result, not a technique improvement" (`factcheck_report.md:250-252`). In the BTL champion the reflex stroke barely moves them (`f_open` 0.153, `f_flick` 0.143, i.e. a constant 0.15; `pgP0LRz2.txt`; poses `dribble_v3.w:743-746`) while the three tables drive both finger pairs (mean |net| 0.14 to 0.45, section 6). Fingers are therefore the first candidate to free in a subset keyframe, and the first ablation to run on the tables.
