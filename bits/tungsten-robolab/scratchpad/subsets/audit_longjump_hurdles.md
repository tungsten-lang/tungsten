## Reading guide: what the long-jump champion actually runs

The newest champion (`long_jump_v4_p95_robust96`, `params/long_jump_v4_gear_final2.txt`) sets `j_ik 1` (line 299), `ik_from 1` (line 307) and `j_il 1` (line 3348). Consequences in `lib/robolab/skills/long_jump_run.w`:

- `add_jump_chunks` (`:123-133`) adds the chunk take-off (`takeoff_chunks`), the ik chunk, the il chunk and the flight chunks, but the flow of the champion is: run-up stride chunks -> `<pen>ik` (`ik_chunk`, `:722-741`, followed by `<t>il` when `@il`) -> `<t>il` (`il_chunk`, `:865-870`, `hold_until(Latch.after(60))`, no successor). So the chunk keyframes `board`, `takeoff`, `hang`, `tuck`, `reach`, `absorb`, `sit` (`takeoff_chunks` `:529-568`, `add_flight` `:1006-1073`) are never entered by the champion. Their tunables in the params file (`j_b_*`, `j_to_*`, `j_hg_*`, `j_tk_*`, `j_rc_*`, `j_ab_*`, `j_rv_*`, `j_g_*`, lines 189-290) are dead leftovers there (verified by reading the chunk graph; the arena has not been asked to confirm it, so mark it "by code reading").
- Take-off and landing are open-loop excitation programs of all 32 muscles ("ORACLE-class", `long_jump_run.w:634-655`, README 655-668): per muscle role a quintic contraction target `c, v, a` and a drive `e` on a 30 ms grid (`ik_h 0.03`, `ik_n 19` = 0.54 s take-off program; `il_*` 34 knots), applied by `prog_apply` (`:962-1002`) as `add_drive`, `set_gain(m, g)`, `add_offset(m, c - cmd)` for every muscle `m` (`while m < body.size`). There is no pose, no joint, and no "left out" in that path: a muscle with a zero table row simply gets zero drive and zero target. `Pose#set` semantics (contraction.w:149-169) do not apply to it.
- What still is a keyframe-like, chunk-and-pose thing in the champion: the two-gear run-up (`RunGearSkill`, the `g2_*` twins, section 5), the planner's hip nudge and the final approach (`stride_correction` `:486-506`, `j_st_*`, `j_fin_*`; `j_pn_*` and every `j_pl_*` except `j_pl_step` are dead here because `ik_from 1` starts the program at the penultimate touchdown, section 5), and the ik program's role tables (section 1). Correction by the later sections: the chunk keyframes are dead only for the `j_il 1` champions (`_robust`, `_robust32`, `_robust96` of v4 and the ik family); `long_jump_v4_p95` (`nom3`, `j_il 0`) still enters `hang` to `sit`, and three older champions still run `board` and `takeoff` (sections 3-4).

## 1. Take-off program `<t>ik` (`l_ik` / `r_ik` chunk; the champion's take-off, run-up planner excluded)

- **Definition.** Chunk `long_jump_run.w:722-741` (`Chunk.new(t + "ik", 1.0)`, no goal, so `@goal_kind = :hold`, `chunk.w:66`; `correct(nil, 0.001)`; latch `ik_off` = foot leaves the board), tables read by `ik_tables`/`prog_tables` `:657-667, 700-715`, applied every tick by `ik_apply` `:781-801` -> `prog_apply` `:962-1002`. Champion `params/long_jump_v4_gear_final2.txt`: `j_ik 1` (:299), `ik_h 0.03` (:300), `ik_n 19` (:301, 0.54 s), `ik_td 0.268` (:302), `ik_gap0 1.8768` (:7710 override), `ik_end 0.508` (:304), `j_ik_g 0.4593` (:305, the reflex gain on every muscle; the `0.0841` at :227 is overridden), `j_ik_kdc 1.0` (:306), `ik_from 1` (:307). Tables: `ik_{c,v,a,e,g}<k>_<role>` for 19 knots x 32 roles = 3040 numbers, lines 308-3347.
- **Intent.** The ideal-control take-off of the runner's own penultimate-touchdown state, distilled into knots (`long_jump_run.w:634-655`). The optimiser maximises ballistic distance D with penalties on forward angular momentum (L <= -1.5) and on trunk pitch at take-off outside [-0.5, 0.8] rad; "the plant placement (touchdown leg angle), the strut, the drive, the free-leg swing and the arm action are all free" (`playground/long_jump_limits.py:1-24`).
- **Evidence on which muscles matter.**
  - None per role. The search space is all 32 muscles x 19 knots (`long_jump_limits.py:6-12`), and no ablation of a role or a joint exists: `grep ablat` over README, `scratchpad/lj2/LOG.md` and `out/long_jump_run/LOG.md` hits only sprint text (README 377, 1265, 1684). Unverified which roles matter.
  - The distilled table has no inert role. From `final2` (my own awk over the file, last key wins): every one of the 32 roles has an excitation `e` that spans 0.8 or more between its min and max over the 19 knots (for example `F_toe_pos` 0.054-0.864, `T_rf` 0.004-0.883), and the gap knots `g` reach |1.4-2.0| on every role. The smallest contraction-target swings are the two-joint muscles of the take-off leg, `T_rf` c 0.352-0.552 and `T_ham` c 0.525-0.749; the largest are `F_ankle_pos` 0.43-1.20 and `T_ankle_pos` 0.41-1.25. That mostly says "CMA filled every cell", not "every cell matters": a sep-CMA table from a rugged objective (scratchpad/lj2/LOG.md:81 "the ideal is rugged", ceiling noise about 0.2 m).
  - The only role subset ever selected by evidence in this family is a feedback one: the v3 P95 champion `long_jump_p95_robust96` (`params/long_jump_p95_pn2.txt:3236-3260`) adds swing-foot lag feedback on five roles only, `ik_kf_F_ham +5`, `F_shoulder_flex_pos -5`, `T_ankle_pos -5`, `T_hip_flex_neg -5`, `T_toe_pos -5`, found by greedy forward selection on 119 approaches (`out/long_jump_run/LOG.md:77`, README 716). `final2` has no `ik_kf_*`/`ik_kp_*`/`ik_kc_*` keys at all (grep count 0); the same greedy selection on the v4 captures was "NOT adopted" (`scratchpad/lj2/LOG.md:186`).
  - Gain: `j_ik_g` 0.459 means the contraction targets `c` are live in `final2` (feedback around the program). The v3 P95 champions ran near open loop at 0.03 (README 667), where only the drive `e` mattered.
- **Proposed subset.** A joint-pose keyframe does not describe this program, and the evidence cannot name free joints. If it were re-expressed as three subset keyframes (plant flight end, board touchdown, take-off instant), the pinned set would be the take-off leg hip/knee/ankle/toe + gas/ham/rf (touchdown angle at the board, then strut extension), the free leg hip and knee (the driving knee) and both shoulder/elbow pairs plus spine (angular momentum, L and pitch are in the objective). Nothing is free by evidence: the free-leg ankle/toe and the elbows are the candidates to release, unverified. The honest verdict is "full-vector is what was optimised".
- **Today.** Yes for a masked program, no for a pose. `prog_apply` writes `add_drive`, `set_gain`, `add_offset` for every muscle in one `while m < body.size` loop (`:993-1001`); skipping a role means the muscle keeps the chunk's :hold target (the command at chunk entry), drive 0 and reflex gain 1.0, because `controller.w:651-656` resets drive/gain/offset to 0/1/0 every tick. That differs from `j_ik_g` 0.459 for that muscle, so a skipped role would be stiffer than the tuned one. No `Pose#set` semantics (`contraction.w:149-169`) apply here.
- **Estimate.** Value 2, cost 4 (a masked re-optimisation of 19 knots x 5 channels over the 117 to 128 captured starts, `scratchpad/lj2/capture_set.sh`; the mask is a few lines in `prog_apply` plus `long_jump_limits.py`), risk 4 (the 96-start gain was +0.25 m, p = 0.039, README 877; every program change needs its own robust stage, `scratchpad/lj2/LOG.md:97`). The worthwhile use is dimension reduction of the search, not a better jump.

## 2. Landing program `<t>il` (the champion's flight-and-landing; `hang`/`tuck`/`reach`/`absorb`/`sit` are not entered)

- **Definition.** Chunk `long_jump_run.w:865-870` (`Chunk.new(t + "il", 1.0)`, goal :hold, `hold_until(Latch.after(60))`, no successor, so it is the last chunk of the graph), tables `il_tables :678-692`, clock and apply `il_entered :872-879`, `il_apply :889-928`, then `prog_apply :962-1002`. Champion `params/long_jump_v4_gear_final2.txt:3348-3356`: `j_il 1`, `il_h 0.08`, `il_n 34` (2.64 s of knots), `il_ld 0.584` (the table time of the sand contact), `j_il_g 0.1733` (reflex gain on every muscle), `il_lz 0.8`, `j_il_kdc 1.0`, `il_tg 0.6`, `il_zl 0.4439`. Tables `il_{c,v,a,e}<k>_<role>`, 34 knots x 32 roles x 4 = 4352 numbers, lines 3357-7708. No `il_kp/kd/kc/pr` keys in `final2` (grep count 0), so no sensed residual.
- **Intent.** The legs tuck and reach on a ballistic clock (before the sand the table time is `il_tg` minus the COM's predicted time to fall to `il_zl`, `long_jump_run.w:881-897`), then the landing is absorbed so that the nearest break in the sand is the feet: World Athletics TR 29.9 takes "the nearest break in the sand made by any part of the body (feet, hands, seat, head, a fall back)" up to 2 s after the landing; a torso past 120 deg is a somersault (TR 30.1.3), touching the runway after landing fails (README 488-500).
- **Evidence.**
  - What fails is timing, not posture: "The landing rolls over: a timing problem, not a rotation-control problem" (README 698). With the knots on the ballistic clock, the 3 roll-overs of 32 hand-overs went to 0 for `tg` 0.58-0.66; `tg` 0.70 makes 48 of 112 fresh starts somersault (README 701-705). The table overfits its clock.
  - The table generalises over the take-offs it was learned on (gen-60 table: 3 of 117 hand-overs roll over, `scratchpad/lj2/LOG.md:212`); the somersaults left (6-7 % of fresh starts) "are mostly not the landing table's" but the take-off's weak short flights (LOG:212, README 711-717, 727-728).
  - Per-role motion over the whole table, from `final2` (my awk, `il_c` min to max): the four toe roles move only 0.18-0.28 in contraction over all 34 knots (F_toe_pos 0.636-0.911, F_toe_neg 0.087-0.369, T_toe_pos 0.355-0.539, T_toe_neg 0.433-0.664), against about 0.9-1.1 for knee and ankle roles in the landing part (knots 8-33: F_knee_pos 1.010, T_knee_neg 0.935, F_ankle_neg 1.027, T_ankle_neg 1.103). At the sand contact (knot 7, `il_ld`) the toes are also the one place the two legs disagree (toe_pos 0.774 against 0.494, toe_neg 0.240 against 0.642), while hip/knee/ankle/rf/ham/gas roles of the two legs agree within about 0.12. The elbows swing in the air (knots 0-7, ranges 0.41-0.64) and then barely move in the landing part (0.20-0.26). This is a proxy (how far the optimiser moved a cell), not an ablation: unverified that any of it is irrelevant.
  - A sensed time-to-strike reflex (`il_ctau`, `il_kc_*`) rescued 2 of 5 weak hand-overs, a learned one 13 to 8 of the 56 hardest (README 728-729): the failure class left is "legs still hanging when the toes touch the sand" (README 699-701), i.e. hip flexion and knee of both legs late.
- **Proposed subset.** Two timed keyframes on the existing ballistic clock instead of one 32-role table: (a) tuck/reach (table time 0 to `il_ld`): pinned = both hips, both knees, spine, both shoulders (legs forward, trunk folding, arms thrown forward as the chunk design intends, `long_jump_run.w:29-34`); free = toes and elbows (hold at their entry values, they hardly move), ankles follow the knee through rf/gas/ham; (b) absorb/sit (`il_ld` to the end): pinned = knees and hips (give) and trunk/arms forward; release the four toe roles and the elbows to hold. Hold, not interpolate, for toes and elbows (their c ranges are 0.18-0.28 and 0.20-0.26). The evidence is for "toes and elbows barely move"; it is silent on whether holding them costs marks.
- **Today.** Only as a mask in `prog_apply` (skip a role: the muscle keeps the :hold command of the chunk entry, drive 0, gain 1.0, see section 1). The pose route does exist for the chunk landing (`sym_pose`, `:1141-1152`, sections 4-5) but the champion's landing is learned muscle by muscle.
- **Estimate.** Value 2, cost 4 (a masked landing search on the 117 hand-overs: about 1.5 h per landing retrain, LOG:298), risk 4 (a retrained landing already cost 20 somersaults of 96 when the take-off changed, LOG:298). Not recommended before the take-off is fixed.

## What a left-out joint does on THIS body (needed for sections 3-6)

**CORRECTION, verified late; it overrides the paragraph below and every "hold by omission" for a hip, knee or ankle in sections 3-6, 9.** The v2 humanoid sends muscle geometry to the controller (`playground/body.py:278-279, 329-330`: "Geometry (JOINT/MLEN/MRANGE) is sent to the controller for this body so Pose maps postures onto every muscle"), so `Pose#apply` takes the geometry branch (`contraction.w:136-138, 146-169`), exactly as `audit_runner_sdk.md` B2 says. The long-jump and hurdle bodies are v3 and v4: `HumanoidV3Builder(HumanoidV2Builder)` (`body.py:562`) and `HumanoidV4Builder(HumanoidV3Builder)` (`:772`) inherit `send_geometry = True` (verified), and their 32-muscle layout (rf, ham, gas per leg, toes) is the v2 one. (The v1 body, `hurdle_hs` base trial, is not checked.) Consequences: (1) omitting a toe, an elbow, a shoulder, the spine or a 3D hip joint IS a clean hold (those pairs are monoarticular; the only biarticulars are rf, ham, gas, `body.py:284-288`); (2) omitting a hip, knee or ankle is NOT a hold: rf and ham (hip + knee) and gas (knee + ankle) are computed with the unposed joint at its REFERENCE angle whenever they also cross a posed joint (`contraction.w:146-167`). So the proposals below that hold a free knee or ankle (sections 3, 4, 6.1-6.3) need the value filled in by the skill (current or interpolated), not omission; the holds for toes, elbows, arms and spine stand.

Original paragraph (superseded; I wrote it from `body.w:72` before finding the geometry branch is the live one):

The long-jump and hurdle bodies are pair-per-joint bodies (`body.w:72` "nil for pair-per-joint bodies"), so `Pose#apply` takes the simple branch (`contraction.w:136-144`): only the pos/neg muscle pair of each NAMED joint is written, "the rest of the base vector is kept", and the base is the chunk's start command (`Chunk#goal_from` `chunk.w:216-228`: `goal = start.copy`, `posed = @goal.apply(body, start)`). So a joint that a pose leaves out HOLDS at whatever the command is when the chunk starts, and the biarticular `rf`/`ham`/`gas` muscles are never written by a pose at all (no pose names them). A subset keyframe by omission is therefore expressible today and means "hold"; it does not mean "interpolate between neighbours" (that needs `via`/`knot`, `chunk.w:26-52`, with the joint posed in the via chunk). The toe joints are already left out this way: no flight chunk poses a toe (`sym_pose`, `long_jump_run.w:1141-1150`) and the take-off poses only the take-off foot's toe (`:544`).

- **Today.** Yes, by omission with hold semantics (section above): drop `p.set(fr + "knee", ...)`, `p.set(fr + "shoulder_flex", ...)` and the `toe` line. The hard-coded free ankle (-0.1, 0.0) and elbow 0.5 show the code already does it for other joints. Drives are separate (`drive_vec`) and are not affected by the pose.
- **Estimate.** Value 2, cost 2, risk 3. Only three champions run these two chunks (`long_jump_robust` 1.85 m, `long_jump_v2` 2.47 m, `long_jump_elite_robust` 3.66 m; their params files carry no `j_ik`, checked by grep; the two seeded `long_jump_v4_elite2` baselines, 0.0 and 0.45 m, are the same kind); every ik champion replaces board and takeoff with the ik program (`j_ik 1`), so the newest champions never enter them. A change needs a re-practice of the `j_*` (2400 rollouts, README 641-642); a hold for the free knee changes the swing-through, which the take-off depends on (README 562).

## 8. Bottom line

1. **A joint subset does not describe the champion long jump.** The take-off (`ik`) and landing (`il`) of the newest champions are open-loop excitation tables of all 32 muscles, 3040 and 4352 numbers, found by sep-CMA on oracle captures (`long_jump_limits.py:1-24`), and no per-role or per-joint ablation exists. The only evidence of a muscle subset that matters is a feedback one: five roles picked by greedy forward selection (`params/long_jump_p95_pn2.txt:3236-3260`), a one-sided F_ham (free-leg hamstring) pitch gain that cut the starts with vz below 2.4 m/s from 10 to 1 of 161 mined starts but turned the lean-forward ones into forward-rotating take-offs (`out/long_jump_run/LOG.md:43, 54`; README 713-715), and a hand-set time-to-strike reflex on hip and knee flexors plus ankle dorsiflexors that rescued 2 of 5 weak hand-overs in the harness (LOG:59). Proxy only: toes and (in the landing) elbows barely move in the tables. The pure-oracle programs also do not transfer between bodies (the one-start champion falls on the labelled beyond-human body per the arena seed row of trial `long_jump_v4_beyond`, `scratchpad/lj3/LOG.md`; the earlier `scratchpad/lj3/probe_beyond_unretuned.txt` is RETRACTED: it ran the P95 body on the wrong runway; "the long jump take-off, being oracle-class, does not transfer", README 1939), which is the honest argument for joint-level keyframes: they carry no muscle strengths.
2. **The chunk keyframes are where subsets are expressible today, and they are live only in `long_jump_v4_p95` (flight and landing), `long_jump_robust`, `long_jump_v2`, `long_jump_elite_robust`.** Omission is a clean hold only for toes, elbows, arms and spine; for hip, knee and ankle the geometry branch pulls rf, ham and gas to the reference angle (correction in the section above), so those subsets need the skill to fill the values. The evidence for which joints matter there is indirect (code-driven joints, cross-champion spread, bounds).
3. **The hurdle trail keyframe is the one place where the intent is explicitly a subset** (hip abduction and rotation freed, thigh held back and out, sensed lift on knee, hip and abduction) and it is also where the 0.991 m run fails. Worth a subset experiment on `hurdle_076` with the lead leg held as a timed via-point, at a modest probability of paying off.
4. **Hygiene:** 92 of 111 `j_*` keys in the champion file are dead; a stripped copy would shrink the next search from 111 to 19 tunables (value 1, cost 1, risk 1).
