## How the champion's postures are built (mechanism, verified)

- Every chunk of the one-leg routine is a FULL-VECTOR posture: `MyoSoccerPosture#pose` sets every joint of the geometry, "every joint set, so no muscle falls back to a reference angle for a joint it crosses" (`lib/robolab/skills/myo_soccer.w:~146-215`, class comment before `+ MyoSoccerPosture`; `base_posture` `:762-770` starts from the model reference or, with `kf_ref 1` (h2b.txt:120), from the key-frame values of `MyoSoccerKeyframe.table` `:129-133`, then sets trunk `flex_extension` and stiffness per joint family `stiff_l` 0.0566 / `stiff_t` 0.0377 (h2b.txt:192-193)). `leg(p, side, hip, knee, ankle)` (`:772-776`) writes three sagittal joints; everything else (hip adduction, hip rotation, subtalar, toe/mtp, trunk lateral and axial) stays at the key-frame value unless a posture line sets it.
- The chunk itself is `Chunk.new(name, dur).pose(posture.pose)` plus a per-tick `balance` correction (`myo_soccer.w` `chunk()` `:~820-826`), so a posture is a feed-forward reference and the feedback terms (`cp4`, `tlh`, `fyaw`, `shc_hip`) add joint offsets on top (`myo_soccer_v4.w:657-670`).
- What "left out" would do: `Pose#apply_geometry` (`lib/robolab/contraction.w:146-169`) gives every muscle that crosses a posed joint the contraction it has with the unposed joints at the REFERENCE posture, plus half the largest stiffness of its posed joints; muscles that cross only unposed joints keep the base command (`contraction.w:105-106, 167`), which at the first chunk is the measured contraction (`controller.w:644-649`). So "leave out" is not "don't care" here either; the soccer code avoids the question by never leaving a joint out.
- Champion chunk order (`myo_soccer_v4.w:1007-1020, 1042-1064`): `b_set` (stance, dur `b_t_ramp` 0.05 s, latch: settled) -> `b_shift_l` (stance + `b_shift_add` 0.1865 on the hips, dur `b_t_shift` 0.708 s, latch: swing foot unloaded and capture point over the stance foot) -> `b_lift_l` (`one_leg_posture`, dur `b_t_lift` 0.314 s) -> `b_hold_l` (`hold_posture`, dur 0.2 s, held up to `b_hold` 1000 s or until the fall-catch latch) -> `b_down_l` -> `b_mid_l` -> branch to the next leg. The 310 s champion stands on the left leg the whole time (`bal_l_tot` 308.37, `bal_r_tot` 0.0005; scratchpad/hazard/LOG.md:131-132), so the operative keyframes are `b_set`, `b_shift_l`, `b_lift_l`, `b_hold_l`.


## 1. Swing-leg park (`b_hold_<st>` = `hold_posture`, the `bhold` switch)

- **Definition.** `hold_posture(st)` `myo_soccer_v4.w:679-688` is `one_leg_posture(st)` (`myo_soccer_v3.w:566-574`) plus four deltas on the PARKED swing leg: `bh_dknee`, `bh_dth` (thigh, world angle), `bh_dank`, `bh_dadd` (hip adduction); they are declared only when `bhold` is on (`:381-405`, defaults -0.2 / 0 / 0 / 0). The hold chunk is `chunk("b_hold_"+st, 0.2, ph, :b_hold, ss, ss)` with `ph = hold_posture(st) if @bhold` (`:1054-1056`). Champion values: `bhold 1`, `bh_dknee -0.2` (h2b.txt:225-226; the other three deltas are absent, so 0). Underlying swing-leg values (h2b.txt:28-31): `b_sw_add` 0.0611, `b_sw_ankle` 0.3316, `b_sw_knee` 1.8, `b_sw_th` 0.1735. Commanded hold posture of the swing leg (my arithmetic from `wleg`, `myo_soccer.w:781-782`, and `tilt_step` -0.2800, h2b.txt:210): hip flexion -0.106, knee 1.6, ankle 0.3316, hip adduction +0.033 (key-frame -0.0279 + `b_sw_add`). Swing hip rotation, subtalar and mtp are never commanded: they stay at the key-frame values (`myo_soccer.w:133-137`, `kf_ref 1` h2b.txt:120). Stiffness `stiff_l` 0.0566 on every hip/knee/ankle/subtalar joint (h2b.txt:192).
- **Intent.** Only WHERE the leg parks. The lift keeps the brake's knee 1.8 so set, shift and lift stay bit-identical; the hold drops the knee command to 1.6, which parks the knee at 1.530 rad, in the middle of the band between the wrap tangents of vaslat_r (1.447) and vasmed_r (1.602), 0.083 / 0.073 rad clear (README 2744-2745; LOG:70-75, 122). The old park (1.6757) sat 0.002 rad from recfem_r's tangent, where MuJoCo 3.6.0's `length_circle` can return NaN (`myo_soccer_v4.w:381-395`).
- **Evidence.**
  - The KNEE alone selects the band: tangents of vaslat/vasmed/vasint/bfsh "do NOT depend on the swing hip at all"; only recfem_r moves, 0.11 rad per rad of hip flexion, -0.05 per rad of hip rotation, ~0 for adduction (LOG:121-123).
  - Parked knee versus command: 1.2 -> 1.168, 1.5 -> 1.449, 1.7 -> 1.592, 1.8 -> 1.676, 1.9 -> 1.765 (LOG:75). Free bands 1.35-1.38, 1.515-1.535, above 1.75 (LOG:74; README 2733).
  - `k_leg` 26 -> 34 moves the parked knee 0.019 rad, `b_sw_add` 0 -> 0.12 only 0.003-0.010 rad; "only the knee command chooses the band" (README 2742-2743; LOG:153). All 27 design cells were clear (LOG:151).
  - Thigh (`b_sw_th`) is not inert: 0.25 gives margin -0.044 with no chatter (sd 1.9e-4); 0.1 hovers; 0.0 / -0.1 fall in 2-3 starts; 0.35 and 0.5 fall (0.5 in 4 of 8) (LOG:38, 57; README 2741; soccer4/LOG.md:119-120 "`b_sw_knee` <= 0.8 or `b_sw_th` >= 0.5 fall").
  - Hip adduction: no falls in the sweep from -0.1 to 0.2 (LOG:38, 58), but it sets the inter-leg contact (`b_sw_add` -0.1 gives 155 N against 208-219 N, soccer4/LOG.md:119); the swing shank presses on the stance leg with 204-223 N in the champion (LOG:80-82, 127). Its feedback owner is `sb_ksw` (counterweight, `myo_soccer_v3.w:977`; `sb_ksw` 1.871 h2b.txt:176).
  - Hip rotation is servo-owned: `swing_yaw` (`myo_soccer_v4.w:847-855`, `fyaw 1`, `fy_k` 0.421, `fy_lim` 0.493, h2b.txt:86-88) steers the swing foot's heading through the swing hip's rotation, so the posture's rotation value is only the servo's bias. Measured per-start parked hip adduction -0.056..-0.097 and rotation -0.03..-0.12 differ and do not matter (LOG:25).
  - Swing ankle: no effect on any wrap margin (sensitivity 0, LOG:29); margin slope -0.1 per rad above 0.15 (README 2741); its limit cycle is what jitters the knee by 0.0025 rad sd (README 2729).
- **Proposed subset.** Pinned: swing knee (1.6 command, 1.53 parked) and swing thigh / hip flexion (keep within about 0.1-0.35; 0.25 is the quiet point). Free and kept at the lift's values (hold, do not interpolate and do not release): ankle, hip adduction, hip rotation, subtalar, mtp. "Release" would be wrong for adduction: the 204 N contact (or `sb_ksw`'s feedback bias) then depends on the reference-posture fill (next point). This is what `bhold` already is: a four-delta subset expressed over a full posture where three deltas are zero.
- **Today.** Only as a full-vector posture with copied values, i.e. as it is. Leaving joints out of `Pose` would NOT mean "hold": `Pose#apply_geometry` (`contraction.w:146-169`) evaluates every muscle that crosses a posed joint with the unposed joints at the REFERENCE posture, so a knee-only pose would re-command rectus femoris, the hamstrings and the gastrocnemii at reference hip/ankle angles, and only muscles crossing nothing but unposed joints keep the chunk's start command (`@start = ctl.command.copy`, `controller.w:58`; `chunk.w:218-228`). The hold chunk would need a "keep the previous chunk's joint values" pose helper, which is a few lines in the skill (copy the posture and overwrite), not an SDK change.
- **Estimate.** Value 1, cost 1, risk 2 for restating it as a subset (pinned sw_knee, sw_hip); the champion is 40 / 40 official, so there is nothing left to gain in score. Note for the Bench: hip adduction, rotation and subtalar are not among the 14 sagittal channels, so the Bench cannot show this keyframe's frontal part at all.

### 1a. Addendum to 1: the parked shank is a prop, so the swing-leg joints are not free

The swing shank rests on the stance thigh (`tibia_r` - `femur_l`, 1.1 mm penetration, 190-230 N, 12 of 12 starts; README 2390-2392; LOG:80-82). Searching that contact down HURT: sep-CMA M took it to 94 N and C2 to 25 N, but on 120 validation starts the brake holds 119, M 116, C2 111, and a 10 N snapshot fell on 4 of 8 unseen starts "all four with no inter-leg contact, while the four with 44-73 N stood" (README 2405-2411). So knee, hip adduction and thigh of the parked leg together decide a load-bearing contact; "release" or "interpolate" for any of them is unsafe, and "hold" is the only defensible mode for the free joints.

## 2. Lift `b_lift_<st>` (`one_leg_posture`, both halves in transit)

- **Definition.** `chunk("b_lift_"+st, b_t_lift, one_leg_posture(st), :b_lift, ss, ss).hold_until(Latch.after(b_t_lift))` (`myo_soccer_v4.w:1051-1052`); posture `myo_soccer_v3.w:566-574`; `b_t_lift` 0.3145 s (h2b.txt:33). Stance half (my arithmetic from h2b.txt:8, 13, 25-27, 82, 93, 122): hip flexion -0.1369 + `b_st_hip` 0.1419 = +0.005, knee 0.3248 + `b_st_knee` -0.3 = 0.025, ankle 0.2424 + `b_st_ankle` 0.0373 = 0.280, hip adduction -0.0279 + `b_add` -0.0151 = -0.043, trunk `flex_extension` `fe` -0.3646. Swing half as in section 1 with knee 1.8 (the lift's command; the hold drops it to 1.6).
- **Intent.** Move the COM onto the stance foot (already done by the shift) and fold the swing leg: a 1.5 rad knee flexion in 0.31 s while the stance leg straightens by 0.3 rad at the knee and 0.14 at the hip. The lift is where the routine still fails: "the swing foot leaves the ground while carrying 20-40 % of the weight and throws the capture point 5-6 cm outward" (README 2250-2253); the brake's remaining falls are "in the shift or lift" (README 2771-2773).
- **Evidence.**
  - Round-5 lift search (sep-CMA L, `runL.sh:8`, 8 s proxy, README 2246-2249) moved every posture tunable: `b_st_hip` 0.2529 -> 0.1419, `b_st_ankle` -0.1500 -> +0.0373, `b_add` +0.0008 -> -0.0151, `b_sw_th` 0.2955 -> 0.1735, `b_sw_knee` 1.570 -> 1.8, `b_sw_ankle` 0.151 -> 0.3316, `b_sw_add` 0.114 -> 0.061 (compare `params/myo_soccer_single_leg_310_cp4.txt` with `..._310_lift.txt`). No per-joint ablation of the lift posture exists in README or the logs (unverified beyond this). A search that moves a value by 0.2-0.3 rad while the result stays within noise indicates a flat direction, but this was not tested.
  - `b_st_knee` is exactly -0.3 in the cp4, trans, lift, ramp, shc and h2b files although searches G, H, J2, L and M all listed it (`runG.sh:8`, `runH.sh:8`, `runJ2.sh:8`, `runL.sh:8`, `run9M.sh:8`): it is either clamped or has no gradient (unverified which). Search A had -0.0754 (`sA.txt:28`).
  - Swing knee: 1.0 holds 8 / 8 at 12 s, at or below 0.8 falls (README 2399-2400), so the knee needs "folded enough", not a value; in transit it dwells at the recfem tangent for about 0.3 s (LOG:118-119, 141).
  - The feedback terms override the stance half's rotation, adduction, subtalar and ankle (section 6), so only the stance hip flexion, knee, trunk `fe` and stiffness are pure feed-forward there.
  - Stance hip rotation sits at the joint's external stop after every lift ("the pelvis yaw sits at the stance hip's external stop (-0.7 rad to the foot) in EVERY seed after the lift", soccer4/LOG.md:15-17; README 2137-2140), so its commanded value is inert.
- **Proposed subset.** Pinned: swing knee and thigh (fold and clear), stance hip adduction (with the swing adduction it sets the lateral COM when the foot leaves), trunk flexion. Free, hold at the shift's values: stance ankle, subtalar, hip rotation (servo or stop), mtp. The stance knee and hip are the open question: straightening them is tuned but unablated.
- **Today.** Only as a full-vector posture (as is). A subset would need a "keep the previous chunk's value for the free joints" helper in the skill, because left-out joints re-evaluate multi-joint muscles at the reference posture (`contraction.w:146-169`).
- **Estimate.** Value 2, cost 2, risk 4. The failing 1.7 % per start happen here, and the evidence to choose the free set does not exist; any change re-rolls the chaotic first 0.3 s ("any change of the first 0.3 s re-draws which seeds fail", soccer4/LOG.md:33-36).

## 3. Weight shift `b_shift_<st>` (already a hip-adduction subset written as a full vector)

- **Definition.** `p = stance; hip_adduction_st += b_shift_add; hip_adduction_sw -= b_shift_add` and `chunk("b_shift_"+st, b_t_shift, p, :b_shift, :both, ss).hold_until(unl.after_min(b_t_shift*0.5), b_t_shift + bc_tmax)` (`myo_soccer_v4.w:1046-1050`). Champion: `b_shift_add` 0.1865 (h2b.txt:24), `b_t_shift` 0.7079 (:37), `b_unload` 0.4 (:38), `b_cp_tol` 0.0322 (:14), `bc_tmax` 2.354 (:43). Latch: swing foot load below `b_unload` and the lateral capture point within `b_cp_tol` of the stance foot target (`captured?`, `myo_soccer_v3.w:556-561`); `lat_still?`, `fore_ok?`, `cpo_ok?` are off in the champion (`blv`, `bfore`, `bcpo` absent from h2b.txt).
- **Intent.** Move the COM over the stance foot. Everything except the two hip adductions is the `stance` posture of section 4, so this keyframe is, by construction, one joint pair.
- **Evidence.** `b_shift_add` is feed-forward and pushes the COM the same way whatever the stance width (r = -0.75 between the width and the capture point at the lift; every outboard lift has a stance narrower than 19 cm; README 2436-2439). The `bshc` brake (h2b.txt:56-59: `bsh_ref` -0.02, `bsh_k` 8, `bsh_lim` 0.15; `myo_soccer_v4.w:716-724`) trims ONLY the two hip adductions and fixed 11 of 11 failing starts, broke 0 (README 2450-2461). Waiting is fatal (`bcpo` fixed 0 of 11, broke 13 of 17, README 2440-2442). Sagittal drift in the shift (COM backward 0.1-0.6 m with both ankles at the +0.524 dorsiflexion limit, soccer4/LOG.md:55-57) was NOT fixed by sagittal strategies: `dsx` 100 / 120 with 3 fixed and 3 broken, k 8 worse (94); `bfore` inert (LOG.md:25-27, 58-59).
- **Proposed subset.** Pinned: hip adduction pair (feed-forward plus the brake's trim); everything else "hold the set posture". That is what the code does, so the subset description is exact.
- **Today.** Yes by construction with full vectors (the two `p.set` lines). A left-out pose would NOT hold: muscles crossing hip adduction also cross hip flexion and rotation and would be re-commanded at the reference hip flexion (`contraction.w:146-169`).
- **Estimate.** Value 1, cost 1, risk 1 for the description; real gains here (lateral over-shift 1.7 % per start) need feedback, not a different keyframe.

## 4. Set `b_set` (the robust stance) and its ramp

- **Definition.** `chunk("b_set", b_t_ramp, stance, :b_set, :both, :mid).hold_until(settled.after_min(b_t_set + ramp_extra), b_t_set + ramp_extra + bs_tmax).gain(b_g_set)` (`myo_soccer_v4.w:1010-1014`); posture `stance` (`myo_soccer.w:797-801`) on `base_posture` (`:762-770`). Champion: `b_t_ramp` 0.05 (h2b.txt:35), `b_t_set` 0.612 (:36), hip -0.1369, knee 0.3248, ankle 0.2424 on BOTH legs (:93, :122, :8), `fe` -0.3646 (:82), `stiff_l` 0.0566, `stiff_t` 0.0377 (:192-193).
- **Intent.** The anatomy forces this posture: upright neutral leaves the rectus abdominis at the long end of its range with about 207 N m of passive lumbar flexion, more than the 156 back extensors return (196 N m), so the stance tips the pelvis back (hip extension) and flexes the lumbar spine, thorax vertical, with tonic extensor drive (`myo_soccer.w:44-51`).
- **Evidence.** Hip, lumbar flexion and tone are coupled through the trunk muscles, so a full vector is justified here. The ramp from the reset posture wants to be instantaneous: `b_t_ramp` 0.3 / 0.2 / 0.15 / 0.1 / 0.05 give 83.3 / 91.7 / 94.2 / 96.7 / 97.5 % on 120 training starts, "monotone: the faster the reset posture is replaced by the stance the better" (soccer4/LOG.md:68-71); on validation the honest gain is about +6 % (LOG.md:81-83). Slower ramps (0.45, 0.8, 1.2 s) were worse (LOG.md:26, 58-60). So the interpolation between the reset posture and the stance carries no information; the ramp is a step.
- **Proposed subset.** None. Keep the full vector; the only change worth stating is that the chunk is a step keyframe (duration 0.05 s), not a via-point.
- **Today.** Already so.
- **Estimate.** Value 1, cost 1, risk 1.

## 5. Contingency chunks: `b_down_<st>`, `b_mid_<st>`, `b_end` (pure catch-only; drop candidates for the champion)

- **Definition.** `b_down` = `stance`, `b_t_down` 0.1367 s, latch swing-foot load above `b_load` 0.05, cap 1.0 s (`myo_soccer_v4.w:1057-1058`; h2b.txt:32, 23); `b_mid` = `stance`, 0.3 s chunk with `settled.after_min(b_t_mid)` (`b_t_mid` 0.497, h2b.txt:34) branching to `next_leg` (`:1059-1062`); `b_end` = `stance`, 100 s (`:1017`).
- **Evidence.** The 310 s champion stands on the left leg for 308.37 s and on the right for 0.0005 s (LOG:131-132), with `b_hold` 1000 s (h2b.txt:15) and the catch stop `bc_in` 0.047 (h2b.txt:42; `:1053`), and `bretry`, `bcout`, `broll` absent (off). So in a successful start none of the three chunks runs; they only run after a caught fall. Retry+catch "rescued 0 of 11 failing seeds" (soccer4/LOG.md:51-54).
- **Proposed subset.** None; they are full `stance` postures. Candidates to drop from the DESCRIPTION of the champion (kept in code as the catch path). Not droppable from the code: a caught early fall then has nowhere to go.
- **Today.** n/a. **Estimate.** Value 1, cost 1, risk 3 (removing the catch path changes every failing start).

## 7. The kick sequence of the goal-scoring champion (`soccer_p1`, phase 1, 50 / 50)

### 7a. Kick evidence from `scratchpad/kick/LOG.md` (round 10, agent P; `kick/LOG.md:n`)

- **The kicking hip's adduction is limit-pinned.** It "sits on its limit (+0.5236) from 1.87 s to the contact in the nominal AND the noisy kicks: the aim servo (k_ky) on the adduction offset has no authority" (`kick/LOG.md:57-58`; `myo_kick.w:6-9`); `kc_ak` +-300 changes nothing (16 and 16 of 48, `kick/LOG.md:81-83`). So the adduction value of `swing` / `snap` (and `aim_add` -0.329) is inert: a free joint, limit-held.
- **The kick needs the leg-on-leg contact.** The swing wraps around the plant leg with 1.3-2.6 kN; with the 14 explicit contact pairs off the champion scores 0 / 16 at noise 0, with them 16 / 16 (`kick/LOG.md:59-61, 96-97`). Shifting the plant target sideways 5-19 cm (`plant_dy` 0.065 -> 0.12 / 0.18 / 0.25) "destroys the kick even at noise 0: the run-up, plant and aim are co-tuned" (`kick/LOG.md:60-61`). The kicking keyframes are therefore contact-coupled to the plant leg: "hold the plant leg" is part of the keyframe, not a free remainder.
- **Which joints' precision matters (reset noise x0.1 on a subset of the 48 joints, goals of 16; `kick/LOG.md:88-92`).** Trunk and spine joints only 13, right leg only 5, left leg only 9; right hips 11, right knee angle 13, right ankle+subtalar+mtp 13, the 8 slaved KNEE coupled joints of the right knee 7 (worst single group), left hips 8, left knee 12, left knee coupled joints 8, left feet 12; "all joints but the hips" 4, "all but the knee coupled joints" 5, "knee main + feet + trunk only" 12. So hips (both) and the slaved knee coordinates are the precision-critical set; trunk, feet and the knee's main angle are tolerant. (This is reset-noise sensitivity of the trajectory, an indirect proxy for which posed joints to pin.)
- **The swing has almost no headroom, the failures are decided before it.** An oracle picking the 13 swing parameters per start from 24 variants gains 6 of 48 packs (`kick/LOG.md:84-87`); a settled stance does not start the kick (0 / 8 at noise 0: "the run-up is tuned to the key frame's first 0.4 s", `kick/LOG.md:93-95`).
- **Post-contact keyframes are flat.** With follow/land at the champion, single-parameter scans on 40 good-contact packs: `drv_post` 0.6 / 0.4 / 0.2 / 0 -> 31 / 31 / 30 / 29; `kr_dz` 0.796 ... 0.6 -> 31 / 28 / 24 / 24 / 24; `drv_brake` 0.5 / 0.8 / 1.0 -> 31 / 27 / 27; `t_follow` 0.15 / 0.3 -> 27 / 32; `th_fol` 0.1 / -0.4 -> 29 / 27; `knee_fol` 0.5 / 1.8 -> 26 / 26; `th_land` -0.1 / 0.6 -> 28 / 31; "nothing beyond +1 of 40: the recovery is at a flat local optimum" (`kick/LOG.md:109-112`). The post-kick falls are a flight: both feet carry 0.00 of the weight 0.2 s after the touch because the plant leg is straight (`ks_dz` 0.796 of a 0.795 m leg, an IK clamp) (`kick/LOG.md:106-108`). **Answers to the typical questions.** (a) Swing-leg park: only the knee angle is pinned by the wrap evidence (its band is chosen by the knee alone); hip flexion is pinned loosely by falls outside about 0.1-0.35; hip adduction and rotation are not needed for the wrap hazard, but they are NOT free to release, because the shank presses on the stance thigh with 204-223 N and lowering that load cost 4-9 of 120 starts (README 2405-2411). (b) Stance-leg posture: the shc parameters (`bsh_*`) pin only the two hip adductions of the shift; in single support the stance ankle, subtalar, hip adduction and hip rotation are owned by `cp4` and `tlh` (rotation is cancelled outright by `tl_cancel` 1.0), so their posture values are inert or bias-only; stance hip flexion, knee and trunk `fe` are pure feed-forward and unablated (`b_st_knee` -0.3 never moved).
