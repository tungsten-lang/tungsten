Robolab demos

Every movement here is a sequence of muscle-contraction targets, eased from one keyframe to the next and switched on sensed events (a foot touching down, the bat coming round). A spinal-reflex layer turns the targets into muscle activations; nothing commands joint torques. The controllers are written in Tungsten and run the bodies in MuJoCo and MyoSuite. Colour on the bodies is muscle activation.

Baseball swing

Leg lift and stride with the pitcher's delivery, then one continuous swing. Contact is planned: the bat is sent to the predicted ball with a set velocity instead of passing a via point, and it brakes before the wrist and hips reach their limits, so nothing stops between contact and the finish. The top hand lets go in the follow-through.

How it works

A movement is a list of chunks. Each chunk eases the muscles' contraction targets from where they are to a goal (a posture or a contraction vector), may pass through it without stopping, and ends on a sensed event. Corrections nudge the targets in real time (the bat toward the predicted ball, a foot toward where the body is heading). Every change is a challenger against a frozen champion on the same held-out starts, and takes over only if it wins a paired test.
