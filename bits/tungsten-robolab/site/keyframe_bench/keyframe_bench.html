Keyframe Bench

Drag a joint to set where the body should be at a gait event. A keyframe only has to pin the joints it cares about (the toe at toe-off, say): the others follow the eased motion between the neighbouring keyframes. The strip below the editor shows the whole stride as it stands now, an onion skin that redraws as you edit; hover it to scrub, and the blue figure shows that point of the stride over the pose you are editing. The left step is the mirror of the right.
