The nonrenewable budgets are 161 and 128 for the entire project. The selected mode determines duration and all four resource requirements. A successor must start at or after its predecessor finishes. The 250 in the PSPLIB file is its loose horizon. The competition instance uses the best-known makespan minus one. PSPLIB records 59.
